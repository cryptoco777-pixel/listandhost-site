Request a call

Talk with us for 15 minutes. Leave with a plan.

Tell us about your home and when you are free. We reply within one business day with a time that fits, by phone or video.

What happens on the call

  1. Your home and your goals. What it earns now, what you want from it, and what you are tired of doing.
  2. What the numbers say. We bring nearby homes on your dates and a first read of your pricing.
  3. Which option fits. Full service, co hosting, a launch or remote pricing. If none of them fit, we say so.
  4. Next steps in writing. A written estimate within one business day after the call. No pressure to sign.

We reply by email within one business day. We never share your information. Privacy.

Who the call is for

Owners thinking about renting a home short term, and hosts who already list and want more from it or less work.

What to have ready

If you already host: your last 12 months of nights booked, average nightly rate and payouts. If not: bedrooms, beds and what makes the home special.

What it costs

Nothing. No sales script and no pressure. Prefer email? Write to team@listandhost.com.
