Broken Bow cabin pricing when every cabin has a hot tub

Updated October 18, 2026

Broken Bow still earns the highest nightly rates of any market we cover: $458 on average in AirROI's data. But supply grew 29.5% in a year while revenue grew 0.2%, and 86% of listings now have a hot tub. Winning here takes pricing and listing work, every day. We do that remotely for cabin owners, from Dallas Fort Worth, about three hours away.

Short answer

The average Broken Bow, OK short term rental listed at $458 a night, booked 35% of its nights and took in about $51,710 in yearly revenue (AirROI public market data, Aug 2025 to Jul 2026, read October 16, 2026). A market average is not a promise of what your home will earn. We do not run full service here yet. Co hosting or remote pricing does. Remote pricing means our engine sets the nightly rates and minimum stays for a home in any US market while the owner keeps local cleaning and upkeep.

Guests

What Broken Bow guests expect, and what still stands out

Expected

  • A hot tub. 86.3% of listings have one. Without it a cabin averaged $21,452 a year, against $56,510 with.
  • A fire pit, a game room and room for 10 or more.
  • Pet friendly options for guests who drive in with the dog.
  • Privacy, trees and a view in the first photo.

What still stands out

  • An indoor fireplace: $54,900 a year with one, $24,071 without.
  • A pool: listings with a pool view, 1.2% of the market, averaged $149,728.
  • A listing that answers the questions before they are asked: road, parking, hot tub rules, check in.
  • Weeknight prices that differ from weekend prices.

AirROI compares listings with and without each feature, August 2025 to July 2026. It shows correlation, not a promise.

How the pricing engine works

  • A rate for every night, a year out, with a floor you approve. No price we set goes under it.
  • Event nights priced on their own, set before the crowd books.
  • Minimum stays by season, lowered for short gaps between stays so those nights can still sell.
  • A fill ladder: unsold nights step down on a set schedule as the date gets close, never past the floor.
  • Every change logged and read back from the channel, so what you see is what guests see.

Questions

What Broken Bow owners ask us

Is Broken Bow oversaturated?

Supply grew 29.5% in a year while revenue grew 0.2%, by AirROI's estimates. The average listing still earned about $51,700, the highest of any market we cover, but each cabin now has to earn its bookings. Daily pricing, weeknight fills and a sharper listing are where the difference is.

Should I add a hot tub, a pool or a game room?

A hot tub is the entry ticket: 86.3% of listings have one, and those without averaged $21,452 a year against $56,510 with. A pool is rare and lines up with the top earners. We compare your cabin to its own comparable set before you spend a dollar.

Do you replace my cleaner or my local manager?

We work remotely: pricing, the listing and, with co hosting, guest messages. Your cleaner and hot tub service stay. You stay the host of record and payouts go straight to you.

How do you fill weeknights?

Weeknights are priced apart from weekends, minimum stays drop for short gaps, and unsold nights step down on a schedule as the date gets close, never under the floor you approve.

What does it cost?

Remote work, co hosting and the launch package are quoted in writing after a free look at your listing. No cost to ask.
