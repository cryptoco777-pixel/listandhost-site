Privacy

Updated October 18, 2026

When you request an estimate, an audit or a call, or contact us, we collect the details you send: your name, email, your listing link and information about your property. Our forms do not ask for a phone number. We use them only to reply to you and to prepare your estimate, audit or service agreement. We do not sell your information. We share it with no one outside List & Host except the service providers we need to run the business.

Referrals

When you refer an owner, we also collect the details you give us about that owner: their name, their email, the city of the home and how you know them, and your brokerage. The form asks you to confirm the owner agreed to hear from us. We use the owner’s details only to contact that owner about your referral, and yours only to reply to you and to pay the referral to your brokerage if they sign. If the owner tells us no thanks, we do not contact them again.

Service providers

  • Google. Our forms send your answers to Google Forms, and they are stored in our Google account. Email sent to our addresses arrives in our Google Workspace mailbox. Pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser asks Google for the font files. See Google's privacy policy.
  • GitHub. Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs such as IP addresses.

The site sets no advertising or tracking cookies of its own. When a form says we never share your information, it means we never share it beyond the providers named here.

To ask us to delete your information, email team@listandhost.com.
