What this site is

This site describes the short term rental management services of List & Host and gives owners tools and guides to decide whether to list a home. It is information. Nothing on it is a contract, and you sign nothing here.

Estimates and results

The instant estimator and the checked estimate are estimates built from market data and, where we have it, your own listing record. Each figure shows its basis. An estimate is not a promise of income. Results shown from the homes our team runs are past results, dated and counted as the results page explains, and are not a promise of what your home will earn.

Agreements

Full service is 20% of booking revenue. Booking revenue is the nightly rent plus any extra guest fees, after the platform's host fee. Taxes and cleaning fees are not part of it. Co hosting, the launch package and remote pricing are quoted in writing after a free look. The term, the notice to leave and everything the service covers are put in writing before you sign anything, and the written agreement is what applies between us.

Your information

What we collect through the forms on this site, who else handles it and how long we keep it is on the privacy page.

Outside sources

Guides and market pages name their outside sources with the date each was read. Those sources belong to their publishers and can change after we read them.
