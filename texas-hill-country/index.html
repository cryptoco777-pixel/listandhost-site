Hill Country rental pricing for Wimberley swimming holes and Fredericksburg wine weekends

Updated October 18, 2026

Wimberley and Fredericksburg are about 75 miles apart and book for different reasons. Wimberley guests come for Blue Hole, Jacob's Well and the creeks. Fredericksburg guests come for wineries, Main Street and Enchanted Rock. Both are weekend markets with a lot of competition. We offer remote pricing and listing work to Hill Country owners.

Short answer

The average Fredericksburg, TX short term rental listed at $338 a night, booked 33% of its nights and took in about $37,095 in yearly revenue (AirROI public market data, Aug 2025 to Jul 2026, read October 16, 2026). A market average is not a promise of what your home will earn. We do not run full service here yet. Co hosting or remote pricing does. Remote pricing means our engine sets the nightly rates and minimum stays for a home in any US market while the owner keeps local cleaning and upkeep.

Guests

What Hill Country guests want

Wimberley

  • Creek or river access, and a short drive to Blue Hole or Jacob's Well.
  • A hot tub and a quiet porch for couples. Room for a wedding party for groups.
  • A pool table: $77,469 a year on average with one, $34,317 without, in 4.4% of listings.
  • Creek and river homes also need clear weather plans and alerts for guests. We write them into the guest guide.

Fredericksburg

  • A walk to Main Street, or a quiet spot on the wine road.
  • Couples guesthouses, and homes for wine weekend groups.
  • A dishwasher: $52,091 a year with, $25,774 without. Only 43% of listings have one.
  • A game console: $77,883 with, in 0.8% of listings. Rare extras stand out in a crowded search.

AirROI compares listings with and without each feature, August 2025 to July 2026. It shows correlation, not a promise.

How the pricing engine works

  • A rate for every night, a year out, with a floor you approve. No price we set goes under it.

  • A fill ladder: unsold nights step down on a set schedule as the date gets close, never past the floor.
  • Every change logged and read back from the channel, so what you see is what guests see.

Questions

What Hill Country owners ask us

Wimberley or Fredericksburg: which earns more?

About the same on average over the past year: $36,211 in Wimberley and $37,095 in Fredericksburg, by AirROI's estimates. Wimberley revenue rose 4.4% while Fredericksburg fell 5.4%, and Fredericksburg has more than three times as many listings.

How do I fill weeknights in the Hill Country?

Weekends sell themselves. Sunday to Thursday is where most homes lose money. We price weeknights apart from weekends, lower minimum stays midweek, let short gaps sell and step unsold nights down on a schedule, never under your floor.

When should I set holiday prices in Fredericksburg?

Early. November and December are peak months there, and guests book about 45 days ahead on average. Holiday nights should carry their own price and minimum stay by early fall.

Do you work with my local cleaner?

Yes. In the Hill Country we work remotely: pricing, the listing and, with co hosting, guest messages. Your cleaner keeps the turnovers and your payouts stay with you.
