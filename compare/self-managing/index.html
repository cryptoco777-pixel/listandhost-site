Compare: self managing

Managing it yourself or hiring us: time, money and risk side by side

Plenty of owners should run their own rental. This page shows where self managing wins, where full service wins and the numbers behind both, so you can decide without a sales call.

Checked October 17, 2026. We are a management company, so we have a stake in your answer. Every outside number names its source, listed at the bottom of the page, and every number of ours says what it is based on.

The short answer

Who should do it themselves, and who should not

Self managing is usually better if

  • You live within about 30 minutes of the home and can get there on short notice
  • You have one home, flexible hours and you enjoy hosting
  • You answer guests fast, nights and weekends included
  • You have a cleaner you trust and a backup for the weeks they cannot come
  • Most of your stays are long, so there are few turnovers to run

Full service is usually better if

  • You live far from the home or travel often
  • You work full time, or you own more than one home
  • Your market runs on events and weekends, where a wrong price costs the most
  • You want the income without being on call

Break even

What one hour of your time is worth here

On the $40,000 example, the fee is $8,000 a year. By our estimate it buys back about 200 to 450 hours a year (17 to 38 hours a month, times 12). That works out to roughly $18 to $40 for each hour you no longer spend, before any change in revenue.

The math gets worse for hiring as revenue falls. On a home that books $20,000 a year, also an illustration, the fee is $4,000 and the same hours cost roughly $9 to $20 each. If an hour of your time is worth less than that and you have the hours, self managing keeps the money in your pocket. If it is worth more, or the hours are not there, full service can make sense.

Owner questions

Questions owners ask

Is it cheaper to manage my own Airbnb?

In fees, yes. You pay no management fee and pay with time instead, about 17 to 38 hours a month for one home with about eight stays a month by our estimate. Whether that is cheaper depends on what an hour of your time is worth and how well you price.

When is self managing the better choice?

When you live close to the home, have one home and flexible hours, answer guests fast and have a reliable cleaner with a backup. Then the fee buys back time you do not need to buy.

What does full service with List & Host cost?

20% of booking revenue, with no setup fee. Booking revenue is the nightly rent plus any extra guest fees, after the platform's host fee. Taxes and cleaning fees are not part of it.

Can I manage it myself now and hire help later?

Yes. If your listing is already live, we join it as your host team, so your reviews and booking history stay yours.
