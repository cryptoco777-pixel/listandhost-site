Compare: national managers

A local team with its own pricing engine, or a large national company?

Large national managers bring scale, years of process and big support teams. We bring a small team that runs homes itself and a pricing engine we built and watch every day. Here is how the two models differ, where each one fits and what to ask either one. We compare no company by name; market figures come from a 2026 fee guide.

Checked October 17, 2026. We are a management company, so we have a stake in your answer. Every outside number names its source, listed at the bottom of the page, and every number of ours says what it is based on.

Strengths

What each model does well

Large national companies

  • Work in many states, so one agreement can cover homes in several markets
  • Large support teams and processes built over years
  • Several plan levels. Limited plans that leave guest issues, cleaning and maintenance with the owner run about 10% to 15% across the market (Awning, 2026)
  • Some run their own booking sites on top of the big platforms

A local team like ours

  • The people who price your home run short term rentals every day
  • Our own engine reviews every night for the next 12 months each morning, inside a floor you approve
  • Every price change is logged and read back, so you can see what moved and why
  • You deal with the same small team that runs your home
  • Full service only where we have local people, so someone can get to the home

Our pricing engine

What it does, and what it does not do

Each morning the engine reviews every night for the next 12 months on the homes our team runs. It works from a floor price, weekend and event prices, minimum stays and a fill plan for quiet weeks. It never sets a price below the floor, never touches a booked night and locks event nights so no automatic cut can reach them. Each change is written, read back and logged.

In its first 26 days live, September 21 to October 16, 2026, it logged 133 nightly price changes and 52 minimum stay changes across the homes our team runs (basis: the engine's own change log).

It does not replace judgment. A person checks nearby homes and approves the big moves. It is also not perfect: our October 13 check of nearby homes found open dates at one of our homes priced 11% to 25% under the local median, and we fixed them that day. Our dynamic pricing guide explains the rules in plain words.

Before you sign

Questions to ask any manager, national or local

  1. Who sets my prices, how often is my home reviewed, and can I see the change log?
  2. What exactly is the fee charged on, in writing? Are cleaning fees included?
  3. Is there a setup fee, and are repairs or supplies marked up?
  4. How long is the term, and how much notice do I give to leave?
  5. Who is my one contact, and who covers for them?
  6. Who cleans, and who checks their work?
  7. Can I see a real monthly statement from a home you run?

Owner questions

Questions owners ask

Are national vacation rental companies cheaper than a local manager?

Sometimes. Across the market, full service fees most often land around 18% to 25% of revenue, and limited plans that leave guest issues, cleaning and maintenance with the owner run about 10% to 15%, according to a 2026 fee guide. Compare what each plan covers and what the fee is charged on, not just the percentage.

What is different about a local team with its own pricing engine?

The people who set your prices run homes themselves. Our engine reviews every night for the next 12 months each morning, inside a floor you approve, and logs every change so you can check it. A person approves the big moves.

When is a national company the better fit?

When you own homes in several states and want one agreement, or you want a company with years of history and a large support staff. We are a small team, and we offer full service only where we have local people.

Will you earn more for my home than a national company would?

Nobody can promise that, and we do not. We show our own results with every number explained, and the instant estimator gives a range for your home with the basis for each figure.
