Branson vacation rental pricing for show season, Christmas and the January dip

Updated October 18, 2026

Branson is a family and group market. Shows on the 76 Strip, Silver Dollar City and Table Rock Lake fill big homes and resort condos from spring through Christmas. Then January goes quiet. We offer remote pricing and listing work to Branson owners, so the busy months carry the slow ones.

Short answer

The average Branson, MO short term rental listed at $251 a night, booked 34% of its nights and took in about $25,747 in yearly revenue (AirROI public market data, Aug 2025 to Jul 2026, read October 16, 2026). A market average is not a promise of what your home will earn. We do not run full service here yet. Co hosting or remote pricing does. Guests

Who books Branson, and what they filter for

Big groups

  • Sleeps 10 to 20 for reunions and church groups.
  • A game room, a bunk room and parking for several cars.
  • Short drives to Silver Dollar City and the Strip.

Condo guests

  • Resort pools, a lake view and an elevator for grandparents.
  • A clean kitchen and a washer for a week stay.
  • Clear check in at the resort desk or gate.

What moves the rate

  • A game console: in 5.7% of listings, and linked to 171% more yearly revenue.
  • The basics: listings with a TV averaged $25,874 a year against $8,164 without.
  • A lake view or dock access on Table Rock, said plainly in the title.

AirROI compares listings with and without each feature, August 2025 to July 2026. It shows correlation, not a promise.

Questions

What Branson owners ask us

How do I fill January and February in Branson?

January averaged 17.4% occupancy in AirROI's data, so no price fills it alone. We lower minimum stays, add weekly discounts, step unsold nights down on a schedule and hold a floor you set, so the quiet months add what they can without giving the house away.

Condo or house: do you work on both?

Yes. Resort condos compete on price, pools and views, so pricing and photos carry them. Big houses compete on group size, game rooms and parking, so the listing has to sell the whole group.

When should Christmas prices be set?

By September. Silver Dollar City's An Old Time Christmas runs November 7, 2026 to January 2, 2027, December is a peak month, and Branson guests book about 50 days ahead on average.
