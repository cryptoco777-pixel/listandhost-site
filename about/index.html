About List & Host

We run short term rentals for home owners. Here is who we are and how we count.

List & Host is a short term rental management company that lists homes on Airbnb, Vrbo and Booking.com, prices them every day, answers guests, schedules cleaning and sends a monthly statement. Full service in Fort Worth, the rest of Dallas Fort Worth and the Buffalo River region of Arkansas. Co hosting or remote pricing anywhere else in the United States.

The company

What List & Host is

List & Host is a management company for owners of short term rentals: whole homes, cabins and unique stays that guests book by the night. We take the daily work off the owner. That means the listing and its photos, the nightly price, every guest message, the cleaner's schedule, repairs and the monthly statement. The owner keeps the home, the listing, the reviews and the payouts.

Full service costs 20% of booking revenue. Booking revenue is the nightly rent plus any extra guest fees, after the platform's host fee. Taxes and cleaning fees are not part of it. There is no setup fee and no markup on repairs or supplies. The full fee page is at listandhost.com/pricing.

We are a small owner team. We sign as The Owner Team and publish no personal names, so the only names on this site are the company's and the places we work. Write to team@listandhost.com and a person answers within one business day.

Where

Where we work

Full service, with local cleaners and repair people we schedule, runs in Fort Worth, Arlington, Dallas and the Buffalo River region of Arkansas. Co hosting, where we run the prices, the guest messages and the cleaning schedule, and remote pricing, where we set the prices and minimum stays, run anywhere in the United States. We publish no street address yet and no phone number; email is how owners reach us.

The engine

How our team runs a home every day

Every home we run sits on one system that our team built and uses on its own homes first. Each morning it reads the calendar, the bookings and the market, then sets the night prices inside a floor the owner approved, with higher prices for weekends and event dates and a fill plan for quiet weeks. Every price it writes is read back to confirm it landed. Routine guest notes go out by rule at the right hour; a person answers every real question. Cleaners get the schedule as bookings change. Owners get a statement for their home by the 10th of each month. An online view of the same numbers is being built. The full walk through is on how it works, and an example of the statement and that view is on the owner dashboard page.

Our numbers

Where our numbers come from

Every figure on this site names its period and its basis. Results come from the booking records of the three homes our team runs, shown as of a stated date, with nights booked kept separate from money earned. Market figures come from named public data services with the date we read them, because those services often disagree with each other. Estimates show a low, a likely and a high case, each with its source. None of it is a promise of income. The current results and the method behind them are on the results page.

When a number changes, we change it on its source page and date the change. If you find a figure that is wrong or out of date, write to team@listandhost.com and we will fix it.

Our rules

What we do not do

  • We never promise income. Every number carries its basis and date.
  • We never offer a guest money or a perk for a review.
  • We publish no testimonials, no ratings markup and no client counts.
  • We take no fee on taxes, cleaning fees or nights the owner blocks for their own use.
  • We never hold guest money. Payouts go from the booking platform to the owner.
  • We give no rules, permit, zoning or tax guidance. Owners check those with their city, county and tax advisor.
