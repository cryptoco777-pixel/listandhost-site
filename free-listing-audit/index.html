What we check

The 12 points

The same checks we run on the homes our team runs. Pricing comes first, because that is where most hosts lose the most money.

Pricing

  1. Price against nearby homes. Your next 90 days against comparable homes on the same dates.
  2. Weekend and weekday split. Whether your weekends earn what the market pays.
  3. Event nights and holidays. Concerts, games, festivals and holidays priced as the big nights they are.
  4. Discounts. Last minute, weekly, monthly and early bird discounts, and how they stack.
  5. Minimum stays and gap nights. Rules that leave single nights unsold.

Listing

  1. Fees and the total guests see. Cleaning, extra guest and pet fees against nearby homes.
  2. Title and first lines. Written for what guests type, not clever phrases.
  3. Photos and accuracy. Your first five photos, and anything guests say the listing got wrong.
  4. Reviews. Your rating, what guests repeat, and how you answer.

Setup

  1. Channels and calendar. Airbnb, Vrbo, Booking.com and how they stay in sync.
  2. Booking settings. Instant book, cancellation policy, advance notice and trip length.
  3. Guest messages. Check in steps, checkout, review follow up and how fast questions get answered.

Background on two of the points: Airbnb ranking and Guest Favorite and dynamic pricing. Prefer we run it for you? See co hosting and remote pricing.
