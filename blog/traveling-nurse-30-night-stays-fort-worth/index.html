30 Night Stays for Traveling Nurses in Fort Worth

List & Host team · October 16, 2026

Fort Worth has a large hospital district just south of downtown, and hospitals there, like hospitals across the country, bring in traveling nurses on contracts that run for months. For a home owner, that is a different kind of guest: one booking instead of ten, one clean instead of ten, and a quiet professional who needs to sleep. This guide covers what changes at 28 nights and beyond, how to price a monthly stay, and how to set a home up for a nurse. If you own in the city, our Fort Worth management page shows how we run homes here.

Why nurses come to Fort Worth

The Near Southside, just south of downtown, is the city's medical district. Baylor Scott and White All Saints, Cook Children's Medical Center, Texas Health Harris Methodist Hospital Fort Worth and the JPS Health Network all sit in or next to it, with many clinics and medical offices around them. Hospitals fill staffing gaps with travel nurses, and staffing firms describe 13 weeks as the most common contract length, often with an option to extend for another 13.

A nurse on a 13 week contract needs about three months of housing, close to the hospital, quiet enough to sleep after a night shift, and simple to pay for. That is a fit for a furnished home that would otherwise sit half empty on winter weeknights.

What the federal travel rate says about budgets

The U.S. General Services Administration sets the lodging rate federal employees can be reimbursed when they travel. For Fort Worth and the rest of Tarrant County, the federal year from October 2026 to September 2027 sets lodging at $192 a night and meals and incidentals at $80 a day. The standard rate for places without their own rate is $113 a night.

Travel nurse agencies set their own housing stipends, and those vary by agency and contract, so treat the federal rate as a ceiling for what a traveler might be paid for lodging, not a price to charge. Nurses compare monthly totals. A clean home with utilities and internet included, at a fair monthly price, competes well.

What changes at 28 nights

Airbnb changes a few things once a stay reaches 28 nights. Here is how they line up for a Fort Worth home booked on Airbnb.

A few details behind the table:

  • Long term cancellation. Airbnb's long term policies apply to stays of 28 nights or more. Under Long Term Firm, a guest must cancel at least 30 days before check in for a full refund. After that, the host is paid for nights already spent plus 30 more nights, or for all remaining nights if fewer than 30 are left.
  • Monthly payouts. For stays of 28 nights or more, Airbnb charges the first month up front and usually releases the first payout by the end of the business day after check in, then pays monthly for the rest of the stay.

28 nights or a lease

Some owners cap stays at 28 or 29 nights so every booking runs as a short stay on the platform. Others accept 30 nights or more and sign a written lease. Both are common. Pick one on purpose before the first monthly guest asks.

How to price a monthly stay

Start from what the same nights would earn as short stays, then account for what a monthly guest saves you.

  • Fewer turnovers. For October 2026 our Fort Worth home has 10 stays booked over 24 nights, as of October 16. That is 10 cleans, 10 check ins and 10 reviews to earn. A monthly guest is one of each.
  • Fewer empty nights. A month booked is a month booked. From late July through October 2026, October as booked on October 16, our Fort Worth home sold 26 of 30 weekend nights but only 37 of 70 weeknights. A monthly guest fills the weeknights that short stays leave open.
  • Platform tools. Airbnb lets you set a weekly discount for stays of 7 nights or more and a monthly discount for stays of 28 nights or more.

A real example: in early October 2026 our Fort Worth home carried a 10 percent weekly and a 25 percent monthly discount on Airbnb. A four week stay priced at about $112 a night, about $3,100 before cleaning. Thirteen weeks at that rate would be 91 nights and about $10,200 before cleaning, as an example, not a quote. Compare that with your own short stay history for the same 91 nights, after cleaning and platform fees, before you set your monthly price.

Watch the Stock Show. A 13 week stay that starts in December runs straight through the Fort Worth Stock Show and Rodeo, January 15 to February 6, 2027. Our price floors for those weeks are $299 on weeknights and $379 on weekends, more than double a monthly rate. Price a long stay that crosses the show with that in mind, or start it after the show ends.

Set the home up for a nurse

  • Blackout curtains and the quietest bedroom you have, for day sleep after night shifts.
  • Fast, steady WiFi and a real desk for charting and continuing education.
  • A washer and dryer in the home. Scrubs get washed constantly.
  • Off street parking with a light by the door. Shifts start and end at odd hours.
  • A full kitchen stocked for meal prep: food storage containers, a slow cooker, a good knife.
  • Utilities and internet included in one monthly price.
  • A light clean and linen change every two weeks, offered or included.
  • Drive times from the home to each hospital in the listing, measured at shift change.
  • A clear pet policy decided up front, since some travelers bring a pet.

Most of this matches our guest ready checklist. The differences are sleep, laundry and a workspace.

Where to find nurse guests

  • Airbnb monthly stays. Set a monthly discount, allow stays of 28 nights or more, and mention the hospital district and drive times in the listing.
  • Furnished Finder. A listing site built for traveling nurses and other professionals on assignment.
  • Staffing firms. Ask the agencies that place nurses in Fort Worth whether they keep a list of local furnished housing.

Screening and paperwork

  • Ask for the hospital name and the contract start and end dates. Match the stay to the contract, with a clear way to extend.
  • For stays of 30 nights or more, use a written lease, take a security deposit, and photograph the home at move in and move out.
  • Keep every message on the booking platform or in writing.

Quick answers

When does Airbnb treat a stay as monthly?

At 28 nights. Monthly discounts, long term cancellation policies and monthly payouts all start at 28 nights.

How long are travel nurse contracts?

Staffing firms describe 13 weeks as the most common length, and many contracts can be extended.
