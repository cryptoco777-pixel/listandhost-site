Why they disagree on the season: AirROI's year includes the nine World Cup matches at the Arlington stadium, June 14 to July 14, 2026, which lifted that summer far above a normal one. Airbtics points to fall, and fall has also been the strongest stretch so far at the 3 bedroom home our team runs. Both services agree on one thing: supply is growing faster than revenue. More homes are chasing the same guests.

Who you compete with, per AirROI for the same period:

  • 85.2% of listings are entire homes or apartments, 38.1% have 3 or more bedrooms, and 45.6% sleep 6 or more.
  • 32.9% take only stays of 30 nights or more, so the pool competing for event weekends is smaller than the headline count.
  • The average stay is 6.8 nights, far longer than a weekend. Work trips, medical stays and families between homes are common reasons. Our guide to 30 night stays covers that side of the market.
  • 41.7% of listings carry Airbnb's Guest Favorite label, 57.2% of hosts are Superhosts, and the average rating is 4.81. A strong rating is the entry ticket here, not a stand out.
  • 11.1% of listings are professionally managed and 37.9% have a co host.

Cleaning fees sit on top. AirROI reports that 87.4% of Fort Worth listings charge one, with a median of $126 across all sizes. The 3 bedroom home our team runs charges $110.

A reality check from our own records: that 3 bedroom home, which sleeps 8, averaged $179 a night after discounts from August through October 2026 (October as booked on October 16). That is under both published 3 bedroom figures, because its quiet weeknights sell near a $149 floor instead of sitting empty. Its weekends sell well above the range. Which brings us to the biggest split in this market.

Three things stand out.

  1. Weekends with a big local draw go first. The TCU homecoming weekend was 97% gone ten days out, and the Monster Trucks weekend 89% gone a month out.
  2. The fuller the weekend, the more the leftovers ask. The three fullest weekends had median asks of $1,010 to $1,618; the three with about a third still open sat at $704 to $800. Cheaper homes sell first, so the homes left are the pricier ones. Treat a high leftover median as a sign of demand, not as a price anyone is sure to get.
  3. Holidays and the Stock Show were still about half open two to three months out. One snapshot cannot tell whether those dates book later or simply have more homes chasing them. Next month's scan will show how fast they fill.

The calendar of the 3 bedroom home our team runs shows the same split. On October 17, 2026, it was 64% booked for the next 14 nights, 47% for the next 30, 30% for the next 60 and 28% for the next 90. Across the next 60 nights, 11 of 17 Friday and Saturday nights were booked (65%), against 7 of 43 other nights (16%). Weekends fill early; weeknights fill late or not at all.

What this means for owners

  1. Price weekends first, and early. The busiest weekends were nearly gone ten days out, and at the home our team runs weekend stays booked a median of 44 days ahead. A weekend priced late is often a weekend priced after the best guests have booked elsewhere.
  2. Hold your price when the market is nearly full. When fewer than one in four homes are left for your dates, the homes still open ask far more. That is not the moment to cut.
  3. Treat the Futurity as four weeks of weeknight demand. It runs November 16 to December 12. Weekly pricing and fast replies to multi night inquiries matter more than the weekend rate.
  4. Set your Stock Show prices now. About half the homes in our area were still open for opening weekend three months out, with a median ask of $738 for two nights. Price Stock Show weeknights as event nights, because the show runs daily.
  5. Check what guests actually see. Early bird, weekly, monthly and last minute discounts stack. On October 13, a 15% last minute discount took a $329 nightly price at the home our team runs down to about $280 for guests. Know your final price after every discount.
  6. Compete on the total, not the nightly rate. Guests compare the whole stay. With most listings charging a cleaning fee, a low nightly rate with a high fee can look more expensive than it is.
  7. Earn the rating. With listing counts up 29% in a year by AirROI's count and 41.7% of listings already Guest Favorites, photos, reviews and reply speed decide who gets booked.
  8. Keep weeknights flexible. Weeknight only stays booked a median of 1 day ahead at the home our team runs. A clean home ready on short notice and a reply in minutes win those nights.

Questions owners ask

What is the average nightly rate for a Fort Worth short term rental?

AirROI reports a $225 average and a $165 median for August 2025 to July 2026. Airbtics reports $184 for September 2025 to August 2026. Bigger homes earn more per night: Red Awning shows $285 for a 3 bedroom home, and our estimator uses $225 to $275. All are estimates before cleaning fees.

How much more do weekends cost than weeknights in Fort Worth?

In our October 13, 2026 scan of 3 and 4 bedroom homes near the venues, a two night weekend stay asked about 1.6 times a two night midweek stay. At the 3 bedroom home our team runs, Friday and Saturday nights averaged about 1.7 times other nights from August through October 2026.

When is the 2027 Fort Worth Stock Show?

January 15 to February 6, 2027, 23 days, at the Will Rogers Memorial Center and Dickies Arena. On October 13, 2026, 46% of the homes in our search area were still open for opening weekend.

How far ahead do guests book in Fort Worth?

AirROI puts the average at 38 days. At the home our team runs, weekend stays were booked a median of 44 days ahead and weeknight only stays a median of 1 day ahead.

How often is this report updated?

Every month, with a fresh live scan, updated market figures and the event calendar for the next six months.
