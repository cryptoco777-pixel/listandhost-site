How Much Can I Make on Airbnb per Month? 12 Markets

List & Host team · October 18, 2026

A 3 bedroom home that is open every night books about $2,800 to $4,900 in an average month across the 12 markets in our estimator, before platform fees, cleaning fees and your own costs. The month matters as much as the market: the same likely case runs about $1,300 in a Branson January and about $4,600 in a Branson June. Both come from our estimator's likely case, built on AirROI market data for August 2025 to July 2026 (read October 16, 2026) and split by each market's monthly pattern.

Short answer. Ask for a monthly range, not one number. Fort Worth, the flattest of the 12 markets, swings from about $2,700 in February to about $3,800 in May for a likely 3 bedroom. Branson, the steepest, swings from about $1,300 in January to about $4,600 in June. January, February and September fall below an average month in all 12 markets. City markets spread the year; getaway markets bunch it.

City markets vs getaway markets

City guests come for work, family, games, concerts and events all year. Getaway guests come when the weather and the school calendar allow.

Getaway markets win June (10.4% of the year on average, against 8.3% in the cities after the World Cup adjustment), March and October. The cities hold up better from December through February and in September.

The groups overlap. Arlington swings from 5.6% of its year in January to 10.6% in November, wider than Fredericksburg or Wimberley in the Texas Hill Country. Fort Worth is the flattest of all 12, between 7.0% and 9.9% every month. Its winter holds up best, and the Fort Worth Stock Show runs through those weeks (January 15 to February 6 in 2027).

Among the getaways, the Buffalo River region peaks in June (11.4%) with October close behind (11.3%); Arkansas Tourism lists September and October as the elk rut in Boxley Valley. Eureka Springs is the only market that peaks in October, at 11.9%. Branson has the deepest winter: January and February together bring 7.6% of its year.

What moves a home up within its month

Inside any month, the home and its pricing decide where it lands against the market line.

  1. Weekends and weeknights are two markets. Our October 13, 2026 comp check covered 214 entire homes with 3 or more bedrooms, searched for six adults in west Fort Worth. For Friday October 16 to Sunday October 18, 20 were still open, and the 10 comparable homes on page one asked a median of $1,028 for the two nights, before taxes. For Tuesday October 20 to Thursday October 22, 102 were open, at a median of $463. Homes left open late lean pricier, so the gap is overstated; still, price weekends early. See our DFW weekend and event pricing guide.
  2. Event dates. In Fort Worth, the NCHA Futurity is scheduled for November 16 to December 12, 2026, and the Stock Show for January 15 to February 6, 2027. Price those nights on their own, with minimum stays; see our Fort Worth event calendar for hosts.
  3. Longer stays for winter. Work and medical stays can fill city winters; see 30 night stays for traveling nurses.
  4. A hot tub in getaway markets. AirROI's April 2026 cabin study measured a 7.9% lift in Broken Bow, 8.3% in Blue Ridge and 10.8% in Gatlinburg; our estimator adds 8% to the nightly rate. Our hot tub guide covers the running costs.
  5. Floors before the slow month. Set your lowest price ahead of time, so automatic discounts cannot sell a night for less than it costs; see dynamic pricing for owners.

Questions owners ask

How much does an Airbnb make per month on average?

For a 3 bedroom home open every night, our estimator's likely case averages about $2,800 a month in Arlington to about $4,900 in Broken Bow, before platform fees, cleaning fees and costs. AirROI's average listing of any size earns about $1,900 to $4,300 a month in these markets ($23,139 to $51,710 a year, August 2025 to July 2026), since many listings are smaller or not open every night.

What is the slowest month for an Airbnb?

January or February, in every one of the 12 markets. September is a second dip: it falls below an average month in all 12, lowest in Broken Bow at 5.5% of the year.

Which month earns the most on Airbnb?

It depends on the market. March leads in five of the 12 (Dallas, Glen Rose, Broken Bow, Wimberley and Fredericksburg), June in three (the Buffalo River region, Hot Springs and Branson), May in two (Fort Worth and Granbury), November in Arlington and October in Eureka Springs.

Can I budget with the yearly estimate divided by 12?

Not safely. In Branson, January and February together bring about $2,700 on a likely 3 bedroom year, about $3,300 short of two average months. Budget from the monthly split and set money aside from the strong months.

Why were June and July changed for Fort Worth, Arlington and Dallas?

The nine World Cup matches at the Arlington stadium, June 14 to July 14, 2026, pushed those months far above a normal year. We replaced them with the average of May and August, which lowers the yearly figures by 4% in Fort Worth, 8% in Dallas and 13% in Arlington.
