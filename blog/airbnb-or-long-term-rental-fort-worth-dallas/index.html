Airbnb or Long Term Rental in Fort Worth and Dallas?

List & Host team · October 18, 2026

For most Fort Worth and Dallas homes in late 2026, a long term lease leaves more money each month than a short term rental that books at the market's average pace, once platform fees, cleaning, utilities and furniture come out. A short term rental wins when the home can book about half its nights or more at market rates (45% to 57% of nights, depending on city and size, if you run it yourself), when you want to use the home some weeks, or when a lease would get in the way of a sale.

Below: lease rents by bedroom count from HUD and Zillow, what a short term rental grosses and keeps on our free estimator, the costs and risks both ways, and the break even occupancy. We run short term rentals in Fort Worth, Arlington and Dallas, so weigh our side with that in mind.

The short answer: when each one wins

  • A lease wins when the home would book at or below the market average, when you want the same money every month with few hours, or when you do not want to buy furniture or be on call.
  • A short term rental wins when the home clears the break even line below, when you want to stay there yourself between guests, or when you may sell within a year and want no tenant in the house.
  • Monthly furnished stays sit between the two; see the middle path below.

Time and risk

  • Vacancy. One empty month costs a lease a full month of rent, and the Census figure above says tenants have plenty to choose from. A short term rental spreads the risk across every night.
  • Seasons. A lease pays the same every month. On the estimator's likely case a Fort Worth 3 bedroom books about $2,700 in February and $3,800 in May; a Dallas 3 bedroom swings from $2,400 in February to $3,900 in March. Event weeks run the other way: the Fort Worth Stock Show runs January 15 to February 6, 2027, and our floors for the home we run near the venues those weeks are $299 on weeknights and $379 on weekends. See our event calendar.
  • Wear and damage. Our Fort Worth home collected a $100 damage payment from one guest through Airbnb in July 2026, and $170 in two resolution payments with one September 2026 stay, one of them for extra pets (our booking records, October 2026). Not every loss comes back.
  • Insurance. The Insurance Information Institute said in March 2026 that standard homeowner policies generally do not cover short term renting. Get a written quote for the use you choose.
  • Management. Stays with only weeknights at our Fort Worth home were booked a median of one day ahead (August to October 2026), so someone answers fast every day. Our self managing guide estimates 17 to 38 hours a month for one home with about eight stays.
  • Flexibility. A lease commits the home for its term. A short term calendar can close to new bookings any day, though booked stays still need hosting or a costly cancellation. Our guide for unsold houses covers selling while hosting.

The middle path: 30 night stays

Furnished stays of a month or more mean one turnover instead of ten. In early October 2026 a four week stay at our Fort Worth 3 bedroom priced at about $112 a night, about $3,100 before cleaning, against HUD's $2,284 lease figure for the size. You still carry utilities, furniture and gaps between contracts. The federal lodging rate for Fort Worth, $192 a night from October 2026 to September 2027, is a rough ceiling on traveler lodging pay, not a price to charge. Questions owners ask

Is Airbnb more profitable than a long term rental in Fort Worth or Dallas?

At the market's average pace in 2026, usually not, once costs come out. On our estimator's likely case a Fort Worth 3 bedroom run short term leaves about $1,607 a month, against $2,189 from a lease at HUD's FY2027 rent. A home that books like the top quarter of the market can pass the lease if you run it yourself.

What occupancy does a short term rental need to beat a lease?

At the estimator's likely nightly rates, about 52% to 57% of nights in Fort Worth and 45% to 51% in Dallas if you run it yourself, or 61% to 66% and 52% to 58% with a manager on both sides, as of October 2026.

How much does a 3 bedroom rent for in Fort Worth and Dallas?

HUD's FY2027 Fair Market Rent, in effect since October 1, 2026, is $2,284 a month for the Fort Worth Arlington area and $2,275 for the Dallas area, utilities included. Zillow's typical asking rent for single family homes across the metro was $2,345 in September 2026.

Can I try short term first and lease later?

Yes. Keep the furniture budget small and judge the test on payouts after costs. Going the other way is harder, because a lease commits the home for its term.
