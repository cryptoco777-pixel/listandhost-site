Buying a Buffalo River Region Cabin to Rent: The Numbers

List & Host team · October 18, 2026

In our estimator’s likely case, a cabin in the Buffalo River region grosses about $21,600 a year with one bedroom, $37,700 with three and $86,700 with six or more, before platform fees and costs (AirROI data for the Jasper area, August 2025 to July 2026). On October 18, 2026, Newton County houses for sale asked 7 to 12 times that likely gross at the middle of each bedroom count, so any house you look at has to prove its own numbers. Dated numbers from the two homes our team runs here follow the checks; our service is Buffalo River cabin management.

Ponca sells on high rates with fewer nights. Its average listing earned $98 per open night against $88 around Jasper (AirROI’s revenue per available night), but on about 104 booked nights a year instead of 136, with shorter stays booked about two months ahead. Price those few strong weeks early, hold minimum stays and expect more cleans per night sold. 37.8% of Ponca listings have a hot tub, against 27.8% around Jasper, and Ponca revenue fell 6.2% from the year before, against 3.7%.

Our estimator uses the Jasper area numbers for the whole region, so near Ponca, read its nightly rate as low and its nights booked as high.

Checks before you make an offer

  1. The road. Drive it after rain, and in winter if you can. At the homes our team runs, the drive ends on a steep hill of loose gravel, so every listing and check in message tells guests what vehicle to bring. Ask who keeps the road up after ice.
  2. The water. Many cabins run on a private well. The EPA advises testing one every year for total coliform bacteria, nitrates, total dissolved solids and pH. A general inspection may skip it: the one on the land our team runs excluded the well’s water and the septic system, by its own scope.
  3. The septic, sized for your guest count. The EPA says a household system should be inspected at least every three years and is typically pumped every three to five, more often with heavy use. A new group every few days is heavy use. Ask the inspector whether the tank and drain field fit the guest count you plan to list.
  4. Signal and internet. 96.1% of Jasper area listings have wifi (AirROI). The FCC’s National Broadband Map shows which internet providers report service at an address and the mobile coverage carriers report, but the FCC notes that mobile coverage reflects use outdoors, not indoors. Test inside the house with your own phone; in our experience signal is weak at many homes here.
  5. The guest count. The average Jasper area listing sleeps 5.4 (AirROI), and our October 13 search found 149 entire homes for 8 guests against 481 places for 2. Big homes face less competition, but every extra guest needs a bed, hot water, parking and septic room. Our glass house sleeps 10, and its average group is 4 guests across stays booked through June 2027, as of October 16, 2026.
  6. The records. Ask for 24 months of platform exports and the bank deposits that match, and plan as if the reviews stay with the seller.

What a buyer should not count on

The river. The Buffalo River Chamber says floating on the upper river is mostly limited to spring and early summer, and fall only in a wet year. A park spokesperson told KY3 in July 2022 that the upper river around Ponca tends to dry up in peak summer. A view of the river is not floatable water in August.

Park crowds. The National Park Service counted a record 1,686,348 park visits in 2024 and 1,393,246 in 2025, when May through July brought 49% of visits and September through November 18%. Yet October is the most booked month for rentals in AirROI’s Jasper area data. Cabin guests come for fall color, elk and cool nights.

What does sell. A deck with a view, trails, a hot tub and dark skies. The Buffalo National River became the first International Dark Sky Park in Arkansas in June 2019, according to the National Park Service, and Arkansas Tourism sends elk watchers to Boxley Valley at dawn and dusk.

Questions buyers ask

How much does a Buffalo River region cabin make a year?

In our estimator’s likely case, built on AirROI data for the Jasper area from August 2025 to July 2026, gross runs about $21,600 a year for 1 bedroom, $37,700 for 3 and $86,700 for 6 or more, before platform fees and costs. It is a market estimate, not a promise for any house.

What do houses cost in Newton County right now?

On October 18, 2026, Redfin listed 87 houses for sale there. The middle of the 85 distinct asking prices on its list pages was $370,000, and 37 of the 87 showed a price cut. Its median sale price was $326,769 for the three months ending September 2026.

Is Ponca or Jasper better for a rental?

They are different businesses. From August 2025 to July 2026, AirROI shows Ponca at $364 a night with 28.5% of nights booked, and the Jasper area at $232 with 37.3%. Ponca earns on high rates and fewer nights.

Can I count on the river to fill a cabin?

No. A park spokesperson told KY3 in July 2022 that the upper river around Ponca tends to dry up in peak summer. Sell the deck, the trails, the stars and the hot tub.
