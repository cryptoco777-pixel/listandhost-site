How Much Can an Airbnb Make in Dallas? By Bedroom, 2026

List & Host team · October 18, 2026

A 3 bedroom Airbnb in Dallas that is open every night books about $40,100 a year in a normal year, the likely case in our estimator, with a range of $31,000 to $51,200. That is nightly revenue before cleaning fees and platform fees, built on AirROI's public Dallas data for August 2025 to July 2026 (4,675 active listings) with the World Cup summer taken out. We read AirROI on October 16, 2026 and checked it again, with every other source below, on October 18, 2026. For how we run homes there, see our Airbnb property management in Dallas page.

Short answer. Size sets the nightly rate, and how a home is run sets how many nights it sells. In AirROI's Dallas data, a listing in the top quarter earns about 65% more per open night than the middle listing ($110.70 against $67.00, World Cup months replaced). Bigger homes earn more per night; they do not book more nights.

Why 2026 overstates a normal Dallas year

AT&T Stadium in nearby Arlington hosted nine World Cup matches from June 14 to July 14, 2026, a semifinal among them. AirROI names June as the Dallas peak revenue month, and June stays were booked 56 days ahead on average, against 30 for the year. Any trailing twelve month figure read in late 2026 or early 2027 carries that summer.

Our estimator replaces June and July 2026 with the average of May and August, which lowers the Dallas year by 8%. Left in, the likely 3 bedroom would read about $43,700 instead of $40,100. If someone shows you a Dallas projection built on 2026 data, ask whether the World Cup is in it.

Unadjusted, AirROI shows $41 at the 25th percentile, $73 at the median, $121 at the 75th and $185 at the 90th.

For a home open every night, that gap is about $16,000 a year. AirROI's unadjusted tiers show where it comes from: the top quarter of Dallas listings books 59% of nights or more against 20% or less for the bottom quarter, and charges $302 a night or more against $187 at the median. What the top quarter does differently, in our experience and in AirROI's report:

  1. Prices every night on its own. Event weekends get a floor early; quiet nights step down before they go empty. Dallas guests book 30 days ahead on average, and February stays only 19, so a price set once in spring goes stale. See dynamic pricing explained.
  2. Sells longer stays on purpose. The average Dallas stay is 5.9 nights, and 36.4% of listings take only stays of 30 nights or more. Weekly and monthly discounts fill January and February. Our guide to 30 night stays shows how.
  3. Has the basics long stays need. AirROI counts $26,080 a year on average for Dallas listings with a washer against $8,708 without, and $25,089 with a kitchen against $6,307 without. That is correlation, not cause, but at least nine in ten listings have each, so a home without them starts behind.
  4. Earns the rating. 50.2% of Dallas hosts are Superhosts and 29.0% of listings carry Airbnb's Guest Favorite label. See how Airbnb ranks listings.
  5. Stands out in a growing field. AirROI counts 37.7% more Dallas listings than a year earlier, while its revenue figure fell 6.8%.

Questions owners ask

How much does a 3 bedroom Airbnb make in Dallas?

Our estimator puts a normal year at $31,000 to $51,200 in nightly bookings, $40,100 likely, for a home open all 365 nights, before cleaning and platform fees. BnBCalc's typical 3 bedroom in the broader Dallas market books $44,000 (September 2026 data).

Is an Airbnb profitable in Dallas?

It can be, but the market alone does not decide it. In our likely 3 bedroom case about $26,100 a year is left after platform fees, discounts and a 20% management fee, before your mortgage, utilities, insurance, repairs and supplies. Whether that is a profit depends on those costs. It is an estimate, not a promise.

What is the Airbnb occupancy rate in Dallas?

AirROI puts the average at 38.4% of nights for August 2025 to July 2026, about 38% at the median. Our estimator's likely case slips from 39.3% for a 1 bedroom to 35.8% for 6 bedrooms, with the World Cup summer taken out.

Do 2026 Dallas numbers include the World Cup?

Most do. Nine matches at AT&T Stadium in Arlington from June 14 to July 14, 2026 lifted June and July. Replacing those months with the average of May and August lowers the Dallas year by 8% in our estimator.

How much is a cleaning fee for a Dallas Airbnb?

Our estimator uses $75 for a 1 bedroom up to $335 for 6 bedrooms, scaled from AirROI's Dallas median of $115. BnBCalc's Dallas page shows $73 to $225 for 1 to 4 or more bedrooms (October 2026). Guests usually pay it, and our fee is never charged on it.
