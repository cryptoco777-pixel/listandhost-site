Short Term Rental Management in Dallas and Arlington, 2026

List & Host team · October 18, 2026 · Updated October 19, 2026

Short term rental management in Dallas and Arlington means a local team runs the pricing, guest messages, cleaning and repairs for your home in exchange for a share of booking revenue; our full service fee is 20%, with no setup fee. Three data services put the average or median listing in the two cities at about $24,000 to $41,000 a year in booking revenue, for periods between August 2025 and September 2026, with Arlington's best nights tied to AT&T Stadium and Globe Life Field and Dallas filled by longer work, medical and family stays.

Every figure below says where it came from and when. The service pages are Airbnb property management in Dallas and Airbnb management in Arlington. For Arlington income by bedroom count, see how much an Airbnb makes in Arlington; for Fort Worth, our Fort Worth Airbnb management guide.

Short answer. The gap inside each city is far bigger than the gap between them. In AirROI's data for August 2025 to July 2026, the top quarter of listings booked about $4,200 a month in both cities, against about $2,300 for the median listing. Pricing, photos, reviews and fast answers make most of that difference, and those are the parts a manager runs.

What drives demand in Arlington

Arlington books on the event calendar. AT&T Stadium, Globe Life Field, Texas Live! and Six Flags Over Texas sit within a few miles of each other, and the city counts 16.5 million visitors a year (City of Arlington, July 28, 2026). The stadium sits between downtown Dallas and downtown Fort Worth, so a big game fills homes across the area.

  • AT&T Stadium. Eight Cowboys regular season home games in 2026, plus the Big 12 Championship and the Cotton Bowl Classic. In June and July 2026 it hosted nine World Cup matches, 631,843 fans in all, per the city.
  • Globe Life Field. The Rangers play 81 home games in 2027, opening at home on Thursday, April 1, per the schedule released July 16, 2026. Baseball's labor agreement expires December 1, 2026, and CBS Sports reported on October 8 that a work stoppage is widely expected, so treat 2027 dates as provisional.
  • Six Flags Over Texas and UT Arlington. Summer family trips, and graduation weekends in May and December.

June 2026 set the city's hotel record, $31 million in room revenue, against the old mark of $23.6 million from November 2024, a football month (same city release). In AirROI's data for August 2025 to July 2026, the peak months were June, July and November, at $408 a night on average, and the low months January, February and April, at $195. Guests booked 41 days ahead on average. June stays averaged 3.4 nights, against 5.3 in March: event crowds book short stays. Plan 2027 on a normal football and baseball year.

What drives demand in Dallas

Dallas books on long stays, with big weekends on top. Visit Dallas counted more than 27.7 million visitors in 2024.

  • Work, moves and medical stays. The average stay was 5.9 nights, booked 30 days ahead (AirROI, August 2025 to July 2026). Relocations, project work, the Southwestern Medical District, Love Field and DFW Airport fill weeknights all year.
  • The State Fair of Texas. 2,020,064 visitors in 2025. The busiest day was Saturday, October 11, Texas vs OU weekend, with 193,960, per the fair's own count.
  • Arena nights. Mavericks and Stars games and concerts at American Airlines Center, fall through spring.
  • Seasons. AirROI's peak months were June, March and December, at $295 a night on average, and the low months January, February and September, at $206.

Dallas is the bigger pond, with about five times Arlington's listings in AirROI's count for August 2025 to July 2026. But only 29.3% of them have 3 or more bedrooms, against 40.1% in Arlington, so a family sized Dallas home faces a smaller slice of that supply.

Neighborhoods guests search for

These areas show up in guest searches and in the data services. They describe demand, not a promise for any street.

  • Uptown, Deep Ellum and Bishop Arts, Dallas. AirROI names them the top Dallas neighborhoods. Restaurants and nightlife draw couples and friend trips, mostly on weekends.
  • Lower Greenville, the Medical District and near Love Field, Dallas. Week long work and medical stays, where a desk, fast wifi and a washer matter more than a pool.
  • The Entertainment District, Arlington. On October 17, 2026, Google suggested "near AT&T Stadium", "near Six Flags" and "near Globe Life Field" for Arlington Airbnb searches, so the title, the first photo and the drive time to the gate carry the listing.
  • Around UT Arlington. Graduations and campus visits, close to the stadiums without game day traffic at the door.

Google also suggested "with pool" for both cities that day.

Questions owners ask

What does short term rental management cost in Dallas and Arlington?

Our full service fee is 20% of booking revenue, with no setup fee. Booking revenue is the nightly rent plus any extra guest fees, after the platform's host fee. Taxes and cleaning fees are not part of it. Repairs and supplies are billed at cost with receipts.

How much does an Airbnb make in Dallas or Arlington?

Data services put the average or median Dallas listing at $24,382 to about $41,000 a year and Arlington at $25,379 to $38,533, for periods between August 2025 and September 2026 (AirROI, Airbtics, GuestFavorites). Arlington's figures include the 2026 World Cup summer, so a normal year may land lower.

Is Dallas or Arlington better for a short term rental?

They are different businesses. Dallas runs on steady work and medical stays booked about 30 days ahead; Arlington runs on the stadium calendar, booked about 41 days ahead (AirROI, August 2025 to July 2026). A big home with parking near the stadiums fits Arlington; a well equipped home near Uptown or the Medical District fits Dallas.

Will a home near AT&T Stadium book every game?

No. Each game is its own market. On October 8, 2026, a Cowboys home game night, our Fort Worth home started at $449 and sold the day before at $199. We set a price and minimum stay for each date, then step unsold nights down, never under your floor.

Do you already run homes in Dallas or Arlington?

We offer full service in both from our Fort Worth base. Today the homes our team runs are in Fort Worth and the Buffalo River region of Arkansas, and we publish their results rather than claim Dallas or Arlington ones we do not have.
