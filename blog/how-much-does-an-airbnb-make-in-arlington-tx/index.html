How Much Does an Airbnb Make in Arlington, TX, by Bedroom?

List & Host team · October 18, 2026

In a normal year, our estimator puts an Arlington, Texas Airbnb that is available every night at roughly $19,500 in bookings as a 1 bedroom, $25,300 as a 2 bedroom, $34,100 as a 3 bedroom and $44,600 as a 4 bedroom, counting nightly revenue before cleaning fees and platform fees. Those are its middle cases, built on AirROI's Arlington data for August 2025 to July 2026 with the World Cup summer taken out; across all sizes, AirROI's average Arlington listing took in $25,379 in that same window. We read every source below on October 17, 2026. For how we run homes there, see our Airbnb management in Arlington page.

Short answer. Bedroom count sets the ceiling, but how a home is run decides where it lands. AirROI's top quarter of Arlington listings earns about three times what its bottom quarter earns per open night ($118 against $40). Bigger homes earn more per night; they do not book more nights.

Why 2026 numbers overstate a normal Arlington year

  • Nine World Cup matches. AT&T Stadium hosted nine matches from June 14 to July 14, 2026, including a semifinal. The city counted 631,843 fans across them.
  • A hotel record. Arlington hotels took in a record $31 million in room revenue in June 2026, more than 30% above the previous monthly record of $23.6 million, set in November 2024, per the City of Arlington's July 28, 2026 release.
  • A spike in the data. AirROI names June as Arlington's peak revenue month, with monthly revenue up to $7,340 and nightly rates peaking at $560. Its weakest month, January, dips to about $1,909.

Any trailing twelve month figure read in late 2026 or early 2027 carries that summer. Our estimator replaces June and July 2026 with the average of May and August, which lowers Arlington's yearly figures by 13%, the biggest cut of the three Dallas Fort Worth cities we cover (Dallas 8%, Fort Worth 4%). If someone shows you an Arlington projection built on 2026 data, ask whether the World Cup is in it.

A reality check from a home our team runs

The closest home our team runs with a booking record sits in west Fort Worth: 3 bedrooms, sleeps 8, taking guests since July 2026. It is not in Arlington, so treat it as a nearby check.

  • Nights and rate. From August through October 2026 (October as booked on October 16), it was booked 56 of 92 nights, or 61%, at an average of $179 a night after discounts, before cleaning and platform fees.
  • Per open night. That is about $109 for every night on the calendar, booked or not, between the likely ($94) and high ($119) cases our estimator gives a 3 bedroom in Arlington.
  • Two kinds of nights. A Friday or Saturday night brought $236 on average against $139 for the rest of the week. Weekend stays were booked about six weeks out (a median of 44 days), while weeknight only stays came in a median of 1 day ahead.

Three fall months is a short record, so we do not turn it into a yearly figure. The lesson carries over: price event weekends early, and answer weeknight requests fast. What moves an Arlington home toward the top quarter

  1. Price event dates early and protect them. Load each game, race and holiday when the date is confirmed, with a floor and a two night minimum on big weekends. Our guide to dynamic pricing explains floors and fill plans, and our DFW weekend and event pricing guide shows what happens when event nights are left unprotected.
  2. Sell to the whole group. More than half of Arlington listings sleep 6 or more. Beds for everyone and a driveway that fits several cars belong in the first photos.
  3. Fill January and February with longer stays. They are the weakest months, and the average stay is already 6.2 nights. Our guide to 30 night stays shows how to reach work and medical stays.
  4. Earn the rating. With 36.8% of listings already Guest Favorites, photos, reviews and reply speed decide who gets booked. See how Airbnb ranks listings.
  5. List on more than one channel. Vrbo and Booking.com reach guests Airbnb does not. See Airbnb vs Vrbo vs Booking.com.

Questions owners ask

How much does a 3 bedroom Airbnb make in Arlington, TX?

Our estimator puts a normal year at $25,200 to $43,600 in nightly revenue, $34,100 likely, for a home that sleeps 8 and is available all year. BnBCalc's typical 3 bedroom in the wider Dallas market books $44,000. Both are before cleaning and platform fees, read October 17, 2026.

What occupancy does an Airbnb in Arlington get?

AirROI puts the average at 34.8% of nights for August 2025 to July 2026, about 33% at the median. GuestFavorites shows 45% for January to September 2026. Our estimator uses 33% to 37% as the likely case, depending on size.

Why do Arlington Airbnb income numbers disagree so much?

Each service picks its own listings, period and method, and some report averages while others report medians. Windows that include June and July 2026 also carry the nine World Cup matches at AT&T Stadium, which set a city hotel record.

What are the best months for an Airbnb in Arlington?

With the World Cup summer taken out, AirROI's pattern makes November, December and March the strongest months and January and February the weakest. Football, the holidays, spring break and the Grand Prix drive the strong months.

Does a bigger home make more in Arlington?

Per night, yes. But bigger homes do not book more nights: in our estimate the likely share of nights booked slips from 37% for a 1 bedroom to 33% for 6 bedrooms, and BnBCalc's wider Dallas table slides from 46% to 41%.
