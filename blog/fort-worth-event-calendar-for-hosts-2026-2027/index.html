Fort Worth Event Calendar for Hosts: 2026 and 2027

List & Host team · October 18, 2026

The Fort Worth dates that move short term rental prices between now and the end of 2027 are the Fort Worth Stock Show and Rodeo (January 15 to February 6, 2027), the NCHA Futurity (November 16 to December 12, 2026), the Cowtown Marathon weekend (February 27 and 28, 2027), the NCAA men's basketball rounds at Dickies Arena (March 18 and 20, 2027), TCU home football Saturdays and a full year of Dickies Arena shows. Price those dates early and above a normal weekend: in our October 13, 2026 scan of 3 and 4 bedroom homes near the venues, a two night event weekend asked about 1.5 to 1.7 times a two night midweek stay, and weekends that were nearly sold out asked 2 to 3.5 times.

Below is the calendar we price around, read from official pages on October 17, 2026, and how prices should move for each kind of event. It is the planning sheet behind our Fort Worth Airbnb management; costs, income by bedroom and how to choose a manager are in our Fort Worth owner guide.

How far event dates move prices

Three sets of numbers, each with its own basis:

  • Live asking prices near the venues. On October 13, 2026 we checked Airbnb for 3 and 4 bedroom homes for 6 guests near the Cultural District, TCU and the Stockyards. Two night midweek stays had median totals of $463 and $468, cleaning included. Event weekends with about a third of homes still open asked $704 to $800. The TCU homecoming weekend, 97% gone ten days out, asked a median of $1,618 for the homes left, and the Monster Trucks weekend, 89% gone a month out, asked $1,010. The Stock Show opening weekend asked $738 with 46% of homes still open, 94 days out.
  • What guests paid at the home our team runs. From August through October 2026, its Friday and Saturday nights averaged $236 and other nights $139, after discounts and before cleaning, about 1.7 times.
  • How early guests book. AirROI puts the Fort Worth average at 38 days between booking and arrival (August 2025 to July 2026). At the home our team runs, guests booked weekend stays a median of 44 days out and weeknight only stays a median of 1 day out.

Cheaper homes sell first, so a high leftover median shows demand, not a price every home will get. Every date we checked is in our Fort Worth market report for October 2026.

Stock Show: price 23 nights, not 6

The 2026 show counted 1,012,925 visitors, down 250,995 from about 1.26 million in 2025, in a year with five days of sleet and freezing rain, as reported by Carnival Warehouse on April 17, 2026. What follows for hosts:

  1. The opening weekend is a long weekend. Martin Luther King Jr. Day is Monday, January 18, 2027, and the arena lists the Cowboys of Color Rodeo that day. Price Sunday night, January 17, as an event night.
  2. Weeknights are event nights. At the home our team runs, Stock Show weeknights ask $299 and weekends $379, against $149 to $199 on ordinary weeknights. Asking prices as of October 17, 2026, not results.
  3. Guests plan in the fall. Rodeo tickets for 2027 went on sale September 14, 2026, per the arena's event page. Have prices loaded by then, and load the 2028 dates the day the show posts them.
  4. Plan for weather. Keep ice melt on hand and line up a backup cleaner.

Minimum stays, discounts and the mistakes we made are in our Stock Show and Dickies Arena host playbook.

Dickies Arena: count the shows per weekend

The arena seats up to 14,000 for a concert, per its own page, and on October 17, 2026 its list already ran to November 6, 2027. Our rule: one Saturday show makes a strong weekend; two shows in one weekend, or one act playing two nights, makes a big one. March 2027 stacks up early: Don Omar on Saturday the 6th, The Love of Soul Tour on Saturday the 13th, Greta Van Fleet on Monday the 15th, the NCAA rounds that week and Hans Zimmer on Monday the 22nd. A single Monday show lifts that one night only.

TCU football: Saturdays, homecoming and graduation

Home games fill Friday and Saturday nights together. The three 2026 home games played so far drew 37,600 to 46,139 fans each, per the season page read October 17, 2026. In our October 13 scan, homecoming weekend had only 7 of 214 homes still open ten days out. For 2027, TCU hosts Alcorn State on September 4, Labor Day weekend, and North Texas on September 11, per FBSchedules on April 17, 2026. Its Big 12 home games against Arizona State, Baylor, Houston and Texas Tech had no dates there; add each one the day it is set. Mark commencement too: TCU planned to confer degrees on more than 2,800 candidates over May 8 and 9, 2026.

Cowtown: a runners' weekend

The Cowtown starts and finishes at the Will Rogers Memorial Center. NBC 5 reported that the 2026 race expected about 28,000 runners from all 50 states. Runners book Friday and Saturday nights, so set a 2 night minimum, and offer a later Sunday checkout if your cleaner can manage it: a marathoner who finishes late on Sunday morning wants a shower before the drive home. Jo Koy plays Dickies Arena the same Saturday. The home our team runs asked $319 a night for that Friday and Saturday on October 17, 2026.

NCAA weekends: price for the bracket

When Fort Worth last hosted the men's first and second rounds, in 2022, the arena says they sold out with more than 13,000 fans, $11.5 million in direct spending and 14,000 room nights. The catch is timing: the NCAA names the teams on Selection Sunday, March 14, 2027, four days before the first game.

  • Hold your price for Wednesday through Saturday night until Selection Sunday. Keep fill rules and last minute discounts off those nights.
  • Allow Wednesday arrivals and keep the minimum at 2 nights. A team that loses Thursday goes home Friday.
  • Once the bracket is out, check similar homes every day. Those four days are the booking window for most fans.

The women's gymnastics championships, April 15 and 17, 2027, are in their ninth straight year in Fort Worth; the arena credits the last edition with more than 8,500 visitors and an estimated 12,000 room nights. Teams qualify at regional meets in the weeks before, so price it the same way.

Horse shows: long stays, not weekends

The NCHA Futurity, Super Stakes and Summer Spectacular bring riders, owners and families who stay for days or weeks. They reward a weekly price that beats a hotel, fast replies to multi night inquiries and a clear answer on where a truck and trailer can park. For stays of a month or more, see our guide to 30 night stays in Fort Worth.

Arlington and Dallas dates spill over

Big games at AT&T Stadium fill homes across the area: the Big 12 Championship on Friday, December 4, 2026, the Cotton Bowl quarterfinal on January 1, 2027 and a Cotton Bowl semifinal on Friday, January 14, 2028. Dallas weekends reach Fort Worth too: over the Texas and OU weekend in October 2026, the home our team runs sold one three night stay at $254 a night after discounts, against $169 across the month. A pricing timeline for any event

  1. Six months out: load the calendar. Give every date above a floor and a minimum stay.
  2. Three months out: first comparison. Check what similar homes ask and how many are still open. On October 13, 2026, 94 days before the Stock Show, 46% of homes near the venues were open.
  3. Six to eight weeks out: hold. Weekend guests book now, a median of 44 days ahead at the home our team runs. When fewer than one in four similar homes are left, hold or raise.
  4. Three weeks out: check the price guests see. Discounts stack after your price, and a last minute discount can carry an event night well under the number you set; our guide to DFW weekend and event pricing shows one at our own home. Keep event nights out of automatic cuts.
  5. The final week: fill the gaps. Open any one night gap beside a booking to one night stays.
  6. After the event: write down what sold. The price, the booking date and the share of homes still open set next year's floor.

Questions owners ask

When is the 2027 Fort Worth Stock Show?

January 15 to February 6, 2027, 23 days, at the Will Rogers Memorial Center and Dickies Arena. The opening weekend runs into Martin Luther King Jr. Day on Monday, January 18.

When is the 2027 Cowtown Marathon?

The race's registration page lists the 5K and 10K on Saturday, February 27, 2027 and the half marathon, marathon and ultra on Sunday, February 28. Confirm on the race's own site before you price.

Which NCAA events are at Dickies Arena in 2027?

The men's basketball first and second rounds on March 18 and 20, and the women's gymnastics championships on April 15 and 17. Men's teams are named on Selection Sunday, March 14.

How much more should a Fort Worth home charge on an event weekend?

It depends on how full the weekend already is. In our October 13, 2026 scan, event weekends with about a third of homes still open asked 1.5 to 1.7 times a midweek stay, and nearly sold out weekends asked 2 to 3.5 times for the homes left. Size, look and reviews decide where your home lands.

How far ahead should I set event prices?

As soon as the dates are posted. AirROI puts the Fort Worth average at 38 days between booking and arrival (August 2025 to July 2026). Late reveal events like the NCAA rounds are the exception: hold the price until the teams are known.
