Harrison, AR Short Term Rentals: What Homes Earn

List & Host team · October 18, 2026

A short term rental in Harrison, Arkansas earns less per night than a cabin near Jasper or Ponca, but its bookings are spread more evenly through the year and its guests stay longer. AirROI's Harrison data for August 2025 to July 2026, read October 18, 2026, shows 97 active listings averaging $151 a night, with 37.5% of nights booked and $16,516 a year per listing. For how we run homes here, see our page on property management in Harrison and the Buffalo River region.

Short answer. Harrison is a steadier, lower rate town than the cabin areas. It books the same share of nights as the Jasper area, 37.5% against 37.3%, at about two thirds of the rate. Its slowest month brought in 55% of its best month's revenue, against 40% around Jasper, and stays average 5.1 nights against 3.1. Work, medical and family guests sit beside weekend visitors to the Buffalo region, so 30 night stays deserve a real price here.

The six top earners on AirROI's page took in $37,121 to $57,589 over the same year; four are 2 or 3 bedroom homes, and three name a hot tub in the title. Most of the market is small: of the 93 listings AirROI sorts by size, 57 have 1 or 2 bedrooms.

Peak months are October, May and June, at 44.7% booked, $161 a night and $2,187 a month on average. The low months, January, February and April, run 31.7%, $151 and $1,369. Low season earns 63% of peak here, against 48% around Jasper in our Buffalo River season guide. October is the best single month at 49.9% booked. It lines up with fall color: a foliage forecast issued October 18, 2026 puts peak color on the Buffalo National River around November 4, and our event calendar for the homes our team runs in the region tags October 15 to November 10, 2026 as the color season.

Who stays in Harrison

  • Medical and work stays. North Arkansas Regional Medical Center, on N. Main Street, offers emergency care, surgery, imaging, women's health, pediatrics, cardiology and orthopedics. As of October 17, 2026, AMN Healthcare's job page for Harrison listed a 13 week travel nurse contract in labor and delivery, starting October 31, 2026. A 13 week contract means about three months of housing near the hospital.
  • North Arkansas College. The two year public college had 2,089 students in federal College Navigator data read October 18, 2026. Campus visits, graduations and new staff looking for housing can bring short and monthly stays.
  • Families and team weekends. Arkansas Tourism says the city hosts more than 75 sports tournaments a year, from volleyball, softball and baseball to 7 on 7 football and basketball, plus summer concerts and hot air balloons each fall. Homes that sleep 6 or more, 48.5% of Harrison listings per AirROI, suit those weekends.
  • Travelers on US 65. Harrison sits on US 65 about an hour south of Branson, per Arkansas Tourism. AirROI's top guest home cities are Springfield, Dallas, Little Rock, Harrison and Fayetteville, and no city dominates: Springfield sends 2.6% of guests. Branson itself is a pricier market; the same AirROI page lists nearby Branson West at $268 a night. See our Branson page.
  • Buffalo region visitors. The park headquarters for the Buffalo National River is in Harrison, and the park's directions start from Harrison, south on Highway 7, Highway 43 or US 65. Arkansas Tourism puts the Buffalo River Trail at Pruitt 12 miles from downtown, with the elk herd and waterfalls like Hemmed In Hollow nearby. Sell the trails, waterfalls, elk and scenic drives, never the river: the upper river often runs too low to float in summer and fall.

Nightly stays vs 30 night stays

Harrison already leans long. Stays average 5.1 nights, from 5.2 in September down to 3.4 in October, when fall color brings leisure guests. Of the 97 listings, 22 (22.7%) set a minimum stay of 30 nights or more, against 19.4% around Jasper. Airbnb counts 28 nights or more as a monthly stay: hosts can set a monthly discount at 28 nights, and Airbnb collects and pays out monthly. For budgets, the GSA lodging rate for Harrison is the standard $113 a night for October 2026 to September 2027, a ceiling for federal travelers, not a price to charge.

What guests here filter for

AirROI's Harrison amenity data (August 2025 to July 2026, read October 18, 2026) shows correlation, not cause: homes with more amenities also tend to be larger.

  • WiFi and a kitchen. Listings without WiFi averaged $9,162 a year, against $17,001 with it; without a kitchen, $9,506 against $16,897. Work and monthly guests treat both as basics.
  • A hot tub. Only 16.5% of Harrison listings have one, against 27.8% around Jasper. Those averaged $25,728 a year, against $14,696 without, so a tub stands out here. Our hot tub guide covers the upkeep.
  • Family gear. The 11.3% of listings with a high chair averaged $25,484, against $15,369.
  • Work and medical basics. Blackout curtains, a washer and dryer, a desk, parking near the door, and drive times to the hospital and the college in the listing. See our guest ready checklist.

The review bar is high in AirROI's Harrison data, read October 18, 2026: a 4.92 average rating, 80.4% Superhosts and 76.3% of listings marked Guest Favorite. Only 13.4% allow Instant Book, and winter stays book 23 days ahead against 35 in summer, so a home that books on the spot catches short notice trips. See how Airbnb ranks listings.
