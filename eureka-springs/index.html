Eureka Springs rental pricing for couples, weddings and the October rush

Updated October 18, 2026

Almost half the rentals in Eureka Springs are one bedroom places: 46.5% in AirROI's data. It is a couples town. Weddings, anniversaries, walks downtown and a hot tub at night. Guests

What wins a couples market

How couples choose

  • A hot tub, a view and a king bed in the first three photos.
  • Honest walk times to downtown. The streets are steep, so say so.
  • Parking at the door, with a photo of it.
  • Privacy. No shared walls, no shared yard.

What moves the rate

  • Wifi. About one listing in ten still has none, and those averaged $10,096 a year against $24,676 with it.
  • A pool table: $53,427 with, $22,089 without, in 3.4% of listings.
  • Exercise equipment: $54,583 with, in 1.0% of listings.
  • Wedding ready: room to get dressed, good light for photos and a clear house rule on guests.

AirROI compares listings with and without each feature, August 2025 to July 2026. It shows correlation, not a promise.

How the pricing engine works

  • A rate for every night, a year out, with a floor you approve. No price we set goes under it.

  • Minimum stays by season, lowered for short gaps between stays so those nights can still sell.
  • A fill ladder: unsold nights step down on a set schedule as the date gets close, never past the floor.
  • Every change logged and read back from the channel, so what you see is what guests see.
