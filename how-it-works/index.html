How it works

Software does the daily work. People make the calls.

Every home our team runs is on our own pricing and messaging engine. It checks bookings, messages and prices all day, writes every change through one channel manager and reads it back. A person decides anything that needs judgment. You see all of it.

The engine

What runs your home every day

The same rules run on every home. Your floor price and your approvals set the limits.

Every 30 minutes

Checks every booking, guest message and calendar night on every channel. New bookings, cancellations and questions show up within the half hour.

Every morning

Reviews prices for the next 12 months. Applies your floor, weekend and event prices, minimum stays and a fill plan for quiet weeks, then writes the changes.

Every stay

Sends what each guest needs by rule: check in steps before arrival, a checkout reminder, then a review and a thank you note. Anything else goes to a person.

Hard limits

Never sets a price below your floor. Never touches a booked night. Locks event nights so no automatic cut can reach them. Big moves wait for a yes.

Every change checked

Each price change is logged before it is made and read back after. If the read back does not match, it raises an alert to our team.

A watcher on the watcher

A supervisor checks the engine twice a day, and a daily audit looks for anything that slipped through.
