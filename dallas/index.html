Airbnb management in Dallas, for long stays and big weekends

Updated October 18, 2026

Dallas guests stay a long time: 5.9 nights on average in AirROI's data, against 3.3 in Branson or Hot Springs. They come for work, hospitals, moves and family. Then the State Fair, Texas vs OU and arena nights stack big weekends on top. We offer full service in Dallas from our Fort Worth base.

The average Dallas, TX short term rental listed at $256 a night, booked 38% of its nights and took in about $24,382 in yearly revenue (AirROI public market data, Aug 2025 to Jul 2026, read October 16, 2026). Guests

What Dallas guests look for

How they search

  • By neighborhood: Uptown, Deep Ellum, Bishop Arts, Lower Greenville, near Love Field or near the Medical District.
  • By trip length. Weekly and monthly discounts show up in search results, and Dallas stays run almost six nights.
  • By workspace and wifi, for the work trip.
  • By parking. Street parking in older neighborhoods worries guests, so a driveway or garage belongs in the first photos.

What moves the rate

  • A washer: $26,080 a year on average with one, $8,708 without. Nine in ten listings have one, so missing it hurts.
  • A full kitchen: $25,089 with, $6,307 without. Long stays cook.
  • Rare extras line up with top earners: kids' bikes ($81,317 a year with, in 0.4% of listings) and a kayak ($65,225 with, 0.3%).
  • Pet friendly with a fenced yard, for relocations and long stays.

AirROI compares listings with and without each feature, August 2025 to July 2026. It shows correlation, not a promise.

How our pricing engine works

  • A rate for every night, a year out, with a floor you approve. No price we set goes under it.
  • Event nights priced on their own, set before the crowd books.
  • Minimum stays by season, lowered for short gaps between stays so those nights can still sell.
  • A fill ladder: unsold nights step down on a set schedule as the date gets close, never past the floor.
  • Every change logged and read back from the channel, so what you see is what guests see.
