Fee questions

Straight answers on what it costs

What counts as booking revenue?

Booking revenue is the nightly rent plus any extra guest fees, after the platform's host fee. Taxes and cleaning fees are not part of it. We earn nothing on nights you block for yourself.

Is there a setup fee?

Not for full service. Launch is included: the walkthrough, the listing, photo direction, pricing setup and your cleaner. Items the home needs to be guest ready are billed at cost, after you approve the list.

Do you take a fee on the cleaning fee?

No. Guests pay the cleaning fee on every booking and it pays the cleaner, at cost. Our 20% is worked out on booking revenue only.

Do you mark up repairs or supplies?

No. Repairs and supplies are billed at cost, with the receipts. You set a repair limit when we start, and anything over it comes to you with a quote first.

How and when do I get paid?

The platforms pay you directly, on their own schedules, so guest money never sits with us. By the 10th of each month we send your statement and one invoice for our fee and any costs.

What will I see each month?

By the 10th, a statement and an owner dashboard for your home, with nights booked ahead, upcoming stays, open nights, payouts and costs. Anything that needs your yes comes to you by email when it comes up. See an example dashboard built from the homes our team runs.

How long is the agreement?

The term and the notice to leave are quoted in writing after a free look, before you sign anything. Stays already booked after the end date are honored.

How much will my home earn?

We never promise a number. The instant estimator shows a low to high range for homes like yours, labeled as an estimate with its basis. A person on our team can check it for free.

Do you pay for referrals?

For real estate agents, yes. If an agent refers an owner who signs, we pay $500 to the agent’s brokerage at the owner’s first payout. How agent referrals work.

Where do you offer full service?

Full service in Fort Worth, the rest of Dallas Fort Worth and the Buffalo River region of Arkansas. Co hosting or remote pricing anywhere else in the United States.
