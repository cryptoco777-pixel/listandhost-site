Our mission

Every stay helps feed a child.

We have pledged 10% of each month’s profit, starting with September 2026, to treat and feed children facing starvation. We started this company to run homes well and to do real good with what they earn.

How our giving works

Where it goes

To a proven program that treats children with severe malnutrition through nutrition clinics, therapeutic food and medicine until they recover.

Why monthly

Hunger does not wait for a big check. Giving each month means help goes out as the homes our team runs earn.

Why it matters to owners

When you list with us, your home is part of it. Every booking helps a child somewhere eat.

Why we do it

Everything we have is a gift. Giving back is how we say thank you, and we plan to raise the share as we grow.
