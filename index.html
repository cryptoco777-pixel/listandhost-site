Questions

What owners ask us

What does 20% cover?

Everything it takes to run the rental: listing and photo direction, daily pricing, all guest messages, cleaning scheduling, maintenance coordination, reviews and your monthly statement. Guests pay the cleaning fee. Repairs and supplies are billed at cost with receipts. Booking revenue is the nightly rent plus any extra guest fees, after the platform's host fee. Taxes and cleaning fees are not part of it.

Do I keep control of my home?

Yes. You can block dates for your own use any time, and anything over a set repair amount comes to you for approval first.

How and when do I get paid?

Payouts come to you from the booking platforms, and every month you get a statement showing every booking, fee and cost.

My home is already on Airbnb. Can you take it over?

Yes, and your reviews stay with the listing. We usually improve pricing, photos and the description in the first weeks. If you would rather keep hosting yourself, ask about co hosting or remote pricing.

How long is the agreement?

The term and the notice to leave are quoted in writing after a free look, before you sign anything. Stays already booked after the end date are honored.
