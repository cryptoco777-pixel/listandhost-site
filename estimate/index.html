How it works

How we build the estimate

  • Market data. For each market we use AirROI's public figures for the 12 months from August 2025 to July 2026: average nightly rate, the share of nights booked, revenue per open night by percentile, cleaning fees and the month by month pattern.
  • Size. Each market's average is spread across bedroom counts using that market's own mix of homes. The size scale matches AirROI's bedroom data for Broken Bow, where 2 bedrooms average $323 a night and 4 bedrooms $575.
  • Three cases. Low is the middle listing in the market. Likely is the market average per open night. High is the top quarter.
  • Your home. Guest count, baths, a hot tub and pets move the number a little. Each change is listed with its basis, and the ones that are our own assumptions say so.
  • One fix for Dallas, Fort Worth and Arlington. June and July 2026 held nine World Cup matches at the Arlington stadium. We replaced those two months with a normal summer so the estimate is not inflated.
  • Checked against the homes our team runs. The Fort Worth home booked about $109 per open night from August to October 2026 (October as booked on October 16), between the likely and high cases for its size. The glass house in the Buffalo River region booked about twice the high case for its size over the same months, because it is one of a kind.

Questions owners ask

Is this a promise of income?

No. It is an estimate from market data. Your street, photos, reviews, house rules and how the home is run all move the real number. Send your address for a checked estimate. Buying a home to rent? Read the numbers to check before you buy.

Why is it above the market average listing?

Many listings are not open every night. Owners block dates, take breaks or only list part of the year. The estimate assumes your home is open all year.

Does it include the cleaning fee?

No. Guests pay the cleaning fee and it goes to the cleaner, so the yearly figure leaves it out.

What if my market is not on the list?
