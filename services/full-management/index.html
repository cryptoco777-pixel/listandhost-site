Full service short term rental managementWe list your home. We host your guests. You get paid.

Full service means every part of running the rental is ours: the listing, daily prices, every guest message, cleaners, repairs, reviews and a monthly statement. You approve the floor price, the repair limit and anything big. The booking sites pay you directly.

The fee is 20% of booking revenue, with no setup fee. Full service in Fort Worth, DFW and the Buffalo River region. Co hosting or remote pricing anywhere else in the US.

Who it fits

Owners who want the income without a second job

Full service is built for a whole home in a market where our team runs the cleaning and the repairs itself. If that is not your home, one of the other three offers probably is, and we say so on the call.

Full service is usually right if

  • Your home is in Fort Worth, Arlington, Dallas or the Buffalo River region of Arkansas
  • You live far away, travel, or simply do not want guest messages on your phone
  • Nobody near the home can handle a lockout, a leak or a cleaner who cancels

  • You want one monthly statement with every booking and every cost

Look at another offer if

  • You rent a room inside the home you live in. We take on whole homes only

The fee

One fee, and what it is charged on

Full service

20% of booking revenue

Booking revenue is the nightly rent plus any extra guest fees, after the platform's host fee. Taxes and cleaning fees are not part of it.

  • No setup fee. Launch is included
  • No fee on cleaning fees or taxes
  • No fee on nights you block for yourself
  • Repairs and supplies at cost, with receipts, never a markup
  • Payouts go straight to you from the platforms. Guest money never sits with us
  • If nothing books, we earn nothing

What is billed at cost

Cleaning turns are paid by guests through the cleaning fee on every booking; it arrives in your payout and pays the cleaner at cost. Repairs, supplies and anything the home needs to be guest ready are billed to you at cost, with the receipt, and only after you approve the list. Your own costs, like utilities, insurance and your mortgage, stay yours, the same as with any manager.

The term and the notice to leave are quoted in writing after a free look, before you sign anything. Stays already booked after the end date are honored.

Owner questions

Questions owners ask about full service

What does the fee cover, and what does it not?

It covers everything it takes to run the rental: the listing and photo direction, daily pricing, every guest message, cleaning scheduling and checks, repair calls, reviews, claims and the monthly statement. It does not cover your own costs, like utilities, insurance and supplies, which are billed at cost with receipts. Guests pay the cleaning fee. Booking revenue is the nightly rent plus any extra guest fees, after the platform's host fee. Taxes and cleaning fees are not part of it.

Is there a setup fee or a launch fee?

No. Launch is included in full service: the walkthrough, the listing, photo direction, pricing setup and your cleaner. Items the home needs to be guest ready are billed at cost after you approve the list.

Do I keep control of my home?

Yes. You block your own dates on the shared calendar any time, with two weeks notice so we can plan cleaning. You set a repair limit when we start, and anything over it comes to you with a quote first. A true emergency that puts the home or a guest at risk is handled right away, and you hear about it the same day.

How and when do I get paid?

The platforms pay you directly on their own schedules, so guest money never sits with us. By the 10th of each month we send your statement and one invoice for our fee and any costs paid for your home, with the receipts attached.

My home is already on Airbnb. Can you take it over?

Yes, and your reviews stay with the listing. Your listing stays in your own account and we join it as your host team. We usually improve pricing, photos and the description in the first weeks. If you would rather keep hosting yourself, co hosting may fit better.

How much will my home earn?

We never promise a number. The instant estimator shows a low to high range for homes like yours in your market, with its basis. The checked estimate from the application looks at your street, your listing if you have one, and the homes it competes with, and labels every figure as an estimate.

How long is the agreement, and how do I leave?

The term and the notice to leave are quoted in writing after a free look, before you sign anything. Stays already booked after the end date are honored.

Do you manage homes outside Fort Worth, Dallas, Arlington and the Buffalo River region?

Not full service. Full service needs our own cleaners and people on the ground. Elsewhere in the United States we offer co hosting and remote pricing, where you keep your local cleaner and we run the daily work from a distance.
