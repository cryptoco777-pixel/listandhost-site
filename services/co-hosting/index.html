Who it fits

Hosts who have the home covered and want the phone to stop

A co host cannot drive to your home. So co hosting works when someone local already can, and what you want off your plate is the pricing, the messages and the scheduling.

Co hosting is usually right if

  • You already list on Airbnb or Vrbo and have a cleaner you trust
  • You, a neighbor or a handy person can get to the home for a lockout, a leak or a missing key
  • Your prices are flat, or set once in a tool you stopped watching
  • Guest messages wake you up, follow you on vacation, or wait hours for an answer
  • Your home is anywhere in the United States, including outside our full service markets

Look at another offer if

  • Nobody near the home can handle hands on problems. In Fort Worth, Arlington, Dallas and the Buffalo River region, that is full service
  • Your messages are fine and only the prices are the problem: remote pricing is the lighter option
  • The home is not listed yet and you plan to host it yourself: start with the setup and launch package
  • You rent a room inside the home you live in. We take on whole homes only

How it works on the platforms

Access, payouts and what each site allows

Airbnb

You add a co host from your listing and pick the access level: full access, calendar and messaging, or calendar only. We ask for full access so the engine can write prices and we can answer guests. Only the listing owner can set up or change a co host's payout share, so your money stays under your control.

Vrbo and Booking.com

Co host access on Airbnb gives no access to the other sites. For Vrbo and Booking.com you add us as a user on each account, or we connect your listings to one calendar so every channel moves together. We tell you which fits your setup on the call.

Your statement

By the 10th of each month: nights sold, average night, payouts by channel, every price change we made and why, and one invoice for our fee. The booking sites keep paying you directly. Guest money never sits with us.

Owner questions

Questions hosts ask about co hosting

What does an Airbnb co host do?

A co host is someone the listing owner adds to help run the listing. Depending on the access the owner grants, a co host can set prices, update the calendar, edit the listing and message guests. With us, that means daily pricing by our engine, every guest message, the cleaning schedule and your reviews. The owner keeps the listing, the reviews and the payouts.

How much does co hosting cost?

Our price is quoted in writing after a free look: we read your listing and the homes it competes with, then send the price, the term and the notice to leave in writing. For context, one industry glossary puts typical co host pay at 10% to 30% of booking revenue, higher when the co host also handles cleaning and maintenance (Hostaway, 2026). Our full service fee, for comparison, is 20% of booking revenue.

How does a co host get paid on Airbnb?

Airbnb lets the listing owner share part of each payout with a co host, and only the listing owner can set up or change that share. Many owners prefer to keep the full payout and pay a monthly invoice instead. We send a statement by the 10th and one invoice; you choose which way it works for you.

Do I lose my reviews or my Superhost status?

No. Your listing stays in your account, so the reviews, the booking history and any status tied to the listing stay yours. We join as your host team.

Does co hosting work on Vrbo?

Yes, with a different mechanism. Vrbo has no co host button like Airbnb's, so you add us as a user on your account, or we connect your Vrbo and Airbnb listings to one calendar so every channel moves together. We tell you which fits your setup on the call.

Who handles a problem at the home?

You or your local person. A co host cannot drive to the home, so cleaning, repairs and lockouts stay with you. We tell you the moment a guest reports one, and we handle the guest side: the message, the apology, any refund you approve. If nobody near the home can do that, full service in our markets is the right offer.

Can I start with only pricing?

Yes. Remote pricing is the lighter option: our engine prices your home every day and you keep every message. Hosts move from one to the other when they like.
