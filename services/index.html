Onboarding

From signed to live in 14 days

This is the plan for a furnished home. A home that still needs furniture or repairs takes longer, and we tell you how much longer on day one.

Day 0

Signed

Agreement signed, access shared and a kickoff call on the calendar.

Days 1 to 3

Walkthrough

We visit or video tour the home and send one setup list: linens, supplies, safety items and the lock.

Days 3 to 6

Pricing and rules

We set your floor, weekend and event prices, minimum stays and house rules with you.

Days 5 to 9

Photos and listing

Photo shoot directed. Title, description and photo order written for how guests search.

Days 8 to 12

Team and access

Cleaner set up with our checklist. Smart lock or lockbox, guest guide and check in steps ready.

Days 12 to 14

Live

Listings go live on Airbnb, Vrbo and Booking.com on one calendar. The engine starts pricing the next morning.
