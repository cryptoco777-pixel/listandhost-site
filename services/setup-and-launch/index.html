Who it fits

New hosts who want to start right, not start over

Most first listings lose their first months to the same mistakes: a flat price, a title nobody searches for, a cover photo of the wrong room, and no plan for the first reviews. This package is the plan.

The launch package is usually right if

  • Your home is furnished, or nearly, and not listed yet
  • You plan to host it yourself and want the setup done by people who do it every week
  • You are turning a home you own into a short term rental for the first time
  • You live anywhere in the United States, including outside our full service markets
  • You want the prices, rules and messages set once, properly, with the reasons written down

Look at another offer if

  • You want someone else to run it after launch. In Fort Worth, Arlington, Dallas and the Buffalo River region, full service includes the launch at no charge
  • You are already listed and want help with the daily work: co hosting or remote pricing
  • The home still needs furniture or repairs. We can still start, and the free look tells you what the home needs first
  • You rent a room inside the home you live in. We take on whole homes only
What is included

Everything between furnished and your first guest

Six pieces, each one the same step we take on a home our team runs.

1. Walkthrough and setup list

By visit or video. One list for your home: linens and towels for everyone it sleeps, kitchen basics, WiFi, a smart lock or lockbox, smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, a fire extinguisher, and the small things guests write reviews about.

2. Listing copy and photo order

A title and first lines written for what guests type into the search box, a description that answers their questions before they ask, and a photo shot list with the cover photo and the order chosen for how guests scroll.

3. Pricing set up with a plan

A floor price, weekend and event prices, minimum stays, and a launch plan for a listing with no reviews yet. Every number written down with its reason, so you can change it later and know what you changed.

4. Three sites, one calendar

Listings built on Airbnb, Vrbo and Booking.com, connected to one calendar so a night sold on one closes on the others. Instant book, advance notice and trip length set to what the first months need.

5. Rules, guide and messages

House rules and quiet hours, a guest guide for the home, check in steps with photos, and the message templates for every stay: before arrival, at check in, the night before checkout, and the review request.

6. Help finding a cleaner

A cleaning checklist for your cleaner, a turnover plan around check in and checkout times, and help finding one if you do not have one yet. The cleaner and the cleaning fee are yours.

Photography, furniture and supplies are billed at cost with receipts if you need them, and only after you approve the list. The full checklist of what a home needs before the first guest: getting a home guest ready.

The four launch moves it used

  • Price for the first reviews. $89 weeknights and $149 weekends until the cabin has three reviews, with the platform's new listing discount on top.
  • Make it easy to book. Instant book on Airbnb and Vrbo, and same day bookings until 6 PM.
  • Lead with the best photo. The loft as the cover, then the porch swing and the wooded view.
  • Cut what does not sell. After the first booking, $169 weekends had no takers. Two October weekend nights were cut to $149 on October 7 and booked within a week.

Owner questions

Questions new hosts ask about the launch package

How much does the setup and launch package cost?

It is a one time price, quoted in writing after a free look. We look at the home by visit or video first, because a furnished three bedroom and an empty cabin are different jobs. Then you get the price and exactly what it covers in writing. If you later move to full service in one of our markets, the launch is included there at no charge.

Do I need to be in Fort Worth, Dallas or Arkansas?

No. The package runs anywhere in the United States. The walkthrough is by video where we cannot visit, and the photo shoot is directed from a shot list with a local photographer billed at cost if you want one.

Who hosts the home after launch?

You do. Every message template, price and rule is written down with its reason, so you can run it with confidence. If you decide you would rather not, co hosting and remote pricing pick up where the launch left off.

What does my home need before the first guest?

Furniture and linens for everyone it sleeps, WiFi, a smart lock or lockbox, smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, a fire extinguisher, and insurance that allows short term rental. The setup list after the walkthrough names everything for your home. The general version is in the guest ready checklist.

How do you price a listing with no reviews?

Lower than the market for the first few stays, with instant book on and short minimum stays, so the first reviews come fast. Then the price steps up as reviews land. On the cabin loft our team launched in September 2026, that plan took three days to the first booking. Every home and market is different, so the plan for yours is written for yours.

Do you take photos?

We write the shot list and direct the shoot. The photographer is billed at cost, or you take the photos yourself from the list. Many hosts do.

How much will my home earn?

We never promise a number. The instant estimator shows a low to high range for homes like yours in your market, with its basis. The checked estimate from the application looks at your street and the homes it competes with, and labels every figure as an estimate.
