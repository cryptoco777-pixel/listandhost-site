Glen Rose and Granbury rental pricing: dinosaur tracks, a lake and a town square

Updated October 18, 2026

Two small towns southwest of Fort Worth, about an hour from our home base, with two different guests. Glen Rose sells dinosaur tracks, Fossil Rim and the Paluxy River to families. Granbury sells Lake Granbury and its historic square to groups and long weekends. We help owners in both price and list.

Short answer

The average Glen Rose, TX short term rental listed at $234 a night, booked 34% of its nights and took in about $29,437 in yearly revenue (AirROI public market data, Aug 2025 to Jul 2026, read October 16, 2026). A market average is not a promise of what your home will earn. We do not run full service here yet. Co hosting or remote pricing does. Over the past year they averaged about the same: $29,437 in Glen Rose and $28,733 in Granbury, by AirROI's estimates. Granbury gets higher nightly rates and longer stays. Glen Rose has far fewer listings, but its supply grew 21% in a year.

Can I rent to work crews?

Yes. Contract crews come to Glen Rose during refueling outages at the Comanche Peak plant, and to both towns for other projects. They want weekly and monthly rates, truck parking and a full kitchen. We price those stays to fill quiet weeks without blocking your best weekends.

How do you price a lake home in winter?

December to February is Granbury's low season. We lower minimum stays, add weekly discounts and let the fill ladder work, while holding a floor you set.
