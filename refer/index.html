Referral questions

Quick answers

Who can earn the referral?

Licensed real estate agents. The $500 is paid to your brokerage. Anyone can point an owner to our free estimate, but the referral payment is for agents only.

When is the $500 paid?

At the owner's first payout after they sign with us, to your brokerage. We tell you by email when they sign.

What if the owner does not sign?

Then there is no payment, and no hard feelings. We send the owner an estimate and answer questions, and we never pressure anyone.

Will you contact a referred owner without their okay?

No. Send us only owners who agreed to hear from us. We write to them by email, and if they say no thanks, that is the end of it.

Can I refer my own sellers?

Yes. An owner whose home is slow to sell can keep it listed and rent it while it sells, or rent it instead of selling. Your seller stays your client and the listing agreement does not change. If they sign with us, we pay the $500 to your brokerage.

Is this tied to reviews?

No. Referral payments are never tied to reviews or ratings, and we never pay or offer anything for a review.

What does the owner pay?

Full service is 20% of booking revenue with no setup fee. Every cost is on our pricing page.
