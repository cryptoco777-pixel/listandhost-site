Already on the books: 18 more stays, 74 nights and $28,642 in payouts from November 2026 to June 2027.

Payouts are what the platforms pay after their own fees, with taxes a platform passes through left out, counted in the month each payout is due, and rounded to the dollar, so the months can differ from the total by a dollar. They include the guest cleaning fee and come before cleaning and other costs. October and later months are booked, not earned, and a cancellation can still change them. Past results are not a promise of what your home will earn.

By the 10th

Your monthly statement

The dashboard looks ahead. The statement closes the month, line by line. Both arrive by the 10th.

On every statement

  • Platform payouts for the month, with taxes left out
  • Cleaning turns, at cost
  • Repairs and supplies, at cost with receipts
  • Our fee, and exactly what it was taken from
  • What is left for you, before your own bills
  • Every reservation by check in date, with nights, channel and payout

What the fee covers and what is billed at cost is all on our pricing page. How the engine and our team run the home is on how it works.

Illustration only, not a forecast. Guests paid $700 in cleaning fees, which covered the 7 turns. Our fee is taken from nightly revenue, never from cleaning fees or taxes. Your own costs, like utilities, insurance, repairs and supplies, are not shown.
