What the engine did

Adjust fast, fill every gap

During launch the automatic rules stay off for this cabin. Each price move is approved by a person and then written through the engine, which checks the floor, reads the price back and logs it.

Cut what does not sell

$169 weekends had no takers

After the first booking, no weekend night sold at $169. On October 7 we cut October 23 and 24 to $149. They booked within a week.

Gap filling

A two night gap, filled

October 17 and 18 sat between two bookings. On October 4 we allowed one night stays on both. A Vrbo guest booked both nights on October 12, and the cabin now runs 13 nights in a row with no gaps.

Guest messages

Shared land, handled in writing

Every cabin check in message carries the shared land rules and a photo of where to park. When the cabin is booked during a main house stay, the engine sends that guest a short heads up three days before arrival and reads it back.

What comes next

Step the price up as reviews land

The plan once the cabin has three reviews: weeknights back to $109, then $129, each step approved and written through the engine.

How we counted these numbers

Nights and nightly rates come from our booking records for each stay. Average night is what guests paid for the nights after discounts, before cleaning fees, taxes and platform fees. Share of nights is nights booked divided by all nights in the month.

Payouts are what the platforms pay after their own fees. They include the guest cleaning fee and come before cleaning and other costs. Our management fee is never charged on the cleaning fee. Each payout is counted in the month it is due, the day after check in. A payout cut by a refund is counted when it was paid, and taxes a platform passes through are left out. Vrbo payouts not yet deposited are estimated at the platform figure less 3.5 percent until the deposit lands.

Ratings, review counts, Guest Favorite status and response rate were read from the public Airbnb listing on October 16, 2026. Other review counts come from our review records across Airbnb, Vrbo and Booking.com. Every number is as of October 16, 2026. Past results from these homes are not a promise of what another home will earn.
