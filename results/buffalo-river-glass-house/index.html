What the engine did

The moves behind the numbers

Our engine runs a full pricing pass every morning and checks bookings and messages every 30 minutes. Every price change is checked against a floor, read back after it is written, and logged. Big moves go to a person, who approves them with one word.

Event and season pricing

Christmas booked at $799 a night

Holidays and peak dates get their own floors and minimum stays, set months ahead. Christmas nights were booked on October 10 at $799. The two October weekends that sold at $749 a night, October 2 to 4 and October 23 to 25, were booked in August and September, before the engine took over this home's prices on September 21, so we do not count them as its work.

Recovering a cancellation

A lost weekend, sold again in eight days

A free cancellation reopened the October 16 to 17 peak color weekend two weeks out. We stepped the price down in three moves while watching the one similar hot tub home still open nearby. It sold on Vrbo the next morning. On October 6 we also moved the listing to a firm cancellation policy, so a late cancellation no longer means a full refund.

Gap filling

Two single nights that would have sat empty

October 8 and October 18 were single open nights between bookings, too short for the two night minimum. On October 1 the engine dropped the minimum to one night on both. The guest arriving October 9 added October 8, and a Vrbo guest booked October 18 three days ahead. Together they added about $928 in payouts, and the house is now booked 22 nights in a row, October 8 to the October 30 checkout (10 of them booked before September 21).

Price floors

No more nights sold under $200

In September, before the hard floor, low leftover prices and last minute discounts sold 11 nights under $200, three of them at $142. On September 28 we set a hard floor of $349 a night on every channel and capped last minute discounts at 10 percent. Every night booked since then has sold at $314 or more: the floor less the capped discount.

Guest messages

100% response rate, replies within an hour

That is what the public Airbnb listing shows. Check in details go out on their own before arrival, including what to know about the gravel drive in. When the cabin next door is booked during a stay, the engine sends the main house guest a short heads up three days before arrival, reads it back and logs it.

How we counted these numbers

Nights and nightly rates come from our booking records for each stay. Average night is what guests paid for the nights after discounts, before cleaning fees, taxes and platform fees. Share of nights is nights booked divided by all nights in the month.

Payouts are what the platforms pay after their own fees. They include the guest cleaning fee and come before cleaning and other costs. Our management fee is never charged on the cleaning fee. Each payout is counted in the month it is due, the day after check in. A payout cut by a refund is counted when it was paid, and taxes a platform passes through are left out. Vrbo payouts not yet deposited are estimated at the platform figure less 3.5 percent until the deposit lands.

Ratings, review counts, Guest Favorite status and response rate were read from the public Airbnb listing on October 16, 2026. Other review counts come from our review records across Airbnb, Vrbo and Booking.com. Every number is as of October 16, 2026. Past results from these homes are not a promise of what another home will earn.
