What the engine did

Event pricing, a fill ladder and a market check

Our engine runs a full pricing pass every morning and checks bookings and messages every 30 minutes. Every price change is checked against a floor, read back after it is written, and logged. Big moves go to a person, who approves them with one word.

Fill ladder

Quiet nights sell, without a fire sale

Open Friday and Saturday nights 22 to 30 days out step down 5 percent a week, never more than 20 percent under their first price and never under the $149 floor (settings as of October 18, 2026). Until October 17 the step was 10 percent a week on every open night 22 to 35 days out. Under that rule, on September 21, Friday and Saturday October 16 and 17 were still open 25 days out. The engine cut both from $249 to $225. A group of six booked both nights the next day.

Event pricing

A concert weekend priced back up, sold in four hours

The ladder had slid the Friday and Saturday of a November concert weekend at Dickies Arena down to $202. Live comps showed similar homes asking $773 and up for those two nights. We raised both nights to $249 on October 9. They sold on Booking.com about four hours later.

Game nights

Start high, step down, no empty nights

October 8, a Cowboys Thursday night game, was priced at $449. Still unsold five days out, it stepped to $249, then to $199 three days out, and sold the day before. October 7 sold that same morning as a one night stay. The house is booked 23 nights in a row, October 2 to the October 25 checkout.

Market check

29 future nights raised to market

On October 13 a live comp check found our open nights 11 to 25 percent under the local median. We raised 29 future nights, among them Thanksgiving from $225 to $319 and 23 weekend nights from December to April up to $299 or $319. Each one was read back and put on hold so no automatic cut can undo it. It is too early to show results.

Event calendar

Big dates set a year ahead

Floors and minimum stays are already in place for the 2027 Fort Worth Stock Show, with a two night minimum and floors of $299 on weekdays and $379 on weekends. Thanksgiving, New Year's Eve and the Cotton Bowl, big Dickies Arena shows and game days, and the night of July 4, 2027 are set too.

How we counted these numbers

Nights and nightly rates come from our booking records for each stay. Average night is what guests paid for the nights after discounts, before cleaning fees, taxes and platform fees. Share of nights is nights booked divided by all nights in the month.

Payouts are what the platforms pay after their own fees. They include the guest cleaning fee and come before cleaning and other costs. Our management fee is never charged on the cleaning fee. Each payout is counted in the month it is due, the day after check in. A payout cut by a refund is counted when it was paid, and taxes a platform passes through are left out. Vrbo payouts not yet deposited are estimated at the platform figure less 3.5 percent until the deposit lands.

Ratings, review counts, Guest Favorite status and response rate were read from the public Airbnb listing on October 16, 2026. Other review counts come from our review records across Airbnb, Vrbo and Booking.com. Every number is as of October 16, 2026. Past results from these homes are not a promise of what another home will earn.
