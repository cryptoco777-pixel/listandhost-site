Guests

What Arlington guests look for

How they search

  • By venue. Guests type AT&T Stadium, Globe Life Field or Six Flags, so the title, the first photo and the map pin carry the listing.
  • By group size. Game day groups travel together, so a 4 or 5 bedroom home with beds for everyone sells to the whole group.
  • By parking. Groups arrive in more than one car, so a driveway that fits three cars belongs in the photos.
  • By minutes to the gate. Game day traffic is real, and drive time beats distance.

What moves the rate

  • A theme room: $70,843 a year on average with one, $23,310 without. Only 4.4% of Arlington listings have one.
  • Mini golf or a big backyard game: $101,051 with, $23,739 without, in 2.1% of listings.
  • The basics still decide most of it. Listings with a full kitchen averaged $26,239, against $6,474 without.
  • A pool for summer families, and house rules that keep neighbors on your side.

AirROI compares listings with and without each feature, August 2025 to July 2026. It shows correlation, not a promise.

How our pricing engine works

  • A rate for every night, a year out, with a floor you approve. No price we set goes under it.
  • Event nights priced on their own, set before the crowd books.
  • Minimum stays by season, lowered for short gaps between stays so those nights can still sell.
  • A fill ladder: unsold nights step down on a set schedule as the date gets close, never past the floor.
  • Every change logged and read back from the channel, so what you see is what guests see.
