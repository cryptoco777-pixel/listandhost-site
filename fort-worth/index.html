Airbnb management in Fort Worth: the fee and what it covers

Full service is one flat 20% of booking revenue. Booking revenue is the nightly rent plus any extra guest fees, after the platform's host fee. Taxes and cleaning fees are not part of it. There is no setup fee, guests pay the cleaning fee, and repairs and supplies are billed at cost with receipts.

That covers the whole job:

  • Listing and photo direction on Airbnb, Vrbo and Booking.com
  • Daily pricing with a floor you approve, and event nights priced on their own
  • Every guest message, from the first question to the checkout reminder
  • Cleaners scheduled around every stay, with our own Fort Worth team
  • Maintenance coordination, with a quote first above an amount you set
  • Reviews and a monthly statement with every booking, fee and cost
