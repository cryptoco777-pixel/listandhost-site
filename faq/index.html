Owner questions

20 questions owners ask us

Updated October 18, 2026

The short version: full service is 20% of booking revenue, the booking platforms pay you directly, and you can block your own dates any time. The 20 answers below cover fees, payouts, control, pricing, guests and getting started. If yours is not here, ask it on a short call.

Fees and money

What does it cost, and how do I get paid?

How much does full service cost?

20% of booking revenue. Booking revenue is the nightly rent and any extra guest fees, after the platform's host fee. It leaves out taxes and cleaning fees. We earn nothing on nights you block for yourself. Every fee and cost is on our pricing page.

What is the difference between full service and co hosting?

Full service covers everything, including local cleaners and repairs, for 20%. Co hosting is for hosts who keep their own cleaner and handle anything at the home, while we run the listing, prices and guest messages for a price quoted in writing after a free look. Both run on the same engine.

Is there a setup fee?

Not for full service. Launch is included. If you only want help going live and plan to host yourself, the launch package is a one time price, quoted in writing after a free look.

Who pays for cleaning?

Guests do, through the cleaning fee on every booking. That fee goes to the cleaner.

Do you mark up repairs or supplies?

No. Repairs and supplies are billed at cost, with the receipts.

How and when do I get paid?

The platforms pay you directly, so guest money never sits with us. By the 10th of each month we send your statement and an invoice for our fee.

How much can my home earn?

It depends on the home, the market and the season, so we never promise a number. Ask for a free estimate and we send a low to high range built from comparable listings and public market data, with the basis for every number.

Control and your home

Who stays in control of the home?

Can I still use my home?

Yes. Block your dates on the shared calendar. Any night no guest has booked is yours. We ask for two weeks notice so we can plan cleaning.

Who approves repairs?

You set a repair limit when we start. We suggest $250. Anything over it comes to you with a quote first. A true emergency that puts the home or a guest at risk gets handled right away, and you hear about it the same day.

My home is already listed. Do I lose my reviews?

No. Your listing stays in your own account and we join it as your host team, so the reviews and the booking history stay yours.

How long is the agreement, and how do I leave?

The term and the notice to leave are quoted in writing after a free look, before you sign anything. Stays already booked after the end date are honored.

Pricing and guests

How are prices set and guests handled?

How do you set prices?

Each morning our engine reviews every night for the next 12 months. It works from your floor price, weekend and event prices, minimum stays and a fill plan for quiet weeks. A person checks nearby homes and approves the big moves.

Will you cut my price just to fill nights?

Only inside limits you approve. The engine never sets a price below your floor, never touches a booked night, and locks event nights so no automatic cut can reach them.

How fast do guests get answers?

Every message gets an answer, every day of the week. Check in steps, checkout reminders and thank you notes go out by rule. Questions go to a person on our team.

How do you protect my home?

Clear house rules and quiet hours, the platforms' own guest verification, and a cleaner who checks and photographs the home after every stay. If something is damaged, we document it and file the claim with the platform on time.

Which platforms do you list on?

Airbnb, Vrbo and Booking.com, kept on one calendar so a night sold on one closes on the others. Each one reaches different travelers.

Getting started

How do we get started?

What areas do you serve?

Full service in Fort Worth, the rest of Dallas Fort Worth and the Buffalo River region of Arkansas. Co hosting or remote pricing anywhere else in the United States.

How long until my home is live?

About 14 days from signing for a furnished home. A home that needs furniture or repairs takes longer, and we tell you how long on day one.

What does my home need before the first guest?

Furniture and linens for everyone it sleeps, WiFi, a smart lock or lockbox, smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, a fire extinguisher, and insurance that allows short term rental. We send one setup list after the walkthrough.

How many homes does your team run?

Three today: a glass house and a cabin loft in the Buffalo River region, and a 3 bedroom home in Fort Worth. The glass house is a Guest Favorite rated 4.9. Your home would run on the same engine. We are a small team, so you deal directly with the people who run it. Who we are and how we count every number is on our about page.
